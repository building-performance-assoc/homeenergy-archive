About This Archive

Home Energy Magazine has been the leading publication for the home performance industry since 1984, covering residential energy efficiency, weatherization, HVAC, building science, and green building. This archive is provided by the Building Performance Association (BPA), the national industry association for home performance professionals. BPA acquired Home Energy Magazine, which has since been succeeded by the BPA Journal. This archive preserves over 1,800 articles, blog posts, and letters to the editor — representing two decades of essential industry knowledge — as a free public resource for contractors, raters, building scientists, and homeowners.
