It comes as no surprise that in near future the supply of fossil fuel will be exhausted. Therefore briquette plant manufacturer thinks about the alternative which replaces coal, lignite and other fossil fuels. Biomass briquettes is the best source which replace all these sources and help us to create a green and healthy environment.

  It is important to understand the benefits of briquette press machine because it is a substantial source of energy which is used to heat industrial boilers. Biomass is abundantly found on earth and it is easy to convert waste in to bio fuel. In our daily life thousand tons of divested and loose biomass material is available but we do not know that how to use it so we burnt it, which cause air pollution. 


But biomass briquetting machine through we can convert waste material into bio fuel which replace coal and lignite. The price of the fossil fuel is increasing so everyone can not afford that much price, while biomass briquettes are economical and have high burning efficiency than coal.

Briquettes are produced from agricultural residues, crops and other industrial waste which come under biomass stock. Biomass fuel is also known as white coal as it is eco friendly. Briquettes are flammable cylindrical blocks that are made from waste material.

All the divested material is collected and compressed under high pressure without using ash so it is binder less technology to make solid fuel.

There are many advantages of briquettes like it is opaque fuel so easy in transportation. Briquettes are ecological so we can remove many environmental problems. It reduces pollution and lead towards the greener environment.
