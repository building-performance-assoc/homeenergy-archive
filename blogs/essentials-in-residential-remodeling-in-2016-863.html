Essentials in Residential Remodeling in 2016

By Cody Bollerman · 12.08.2016

It’s no secret that residential remodeling is always changing. As a very cyclical industry some years there are boom and some years are bust which brings some swifts naturally. Items such as material cost fluctuations, regulations, and labor force are integral to remodeling and can have ramifications to what is going on in residential. Here are some core essentials to be aware of in today’s residential remodeling game in 2016.

Qualified Workers

As mentioned before labor pools drastically change in residential remodeling. In good times, everyone and their brother gets into contracting as pre-recession it was much easier to setup shop and make a profit. However, these days with post 7 years being very shakey many construction workers in the past have left to other industries and simply no longer around.

Execution Vs What’s On TV

In today’s insanely interconnected world there is a huge gap in conception versus reality, especially in construction. Unfortunately, as reality TV continues to create remodeling shows and show about 50% of what is really involved inexperienced homeowners will continue to be misled. It is very important to understand a good general contractor will tell you what is necessary to build structurally, pass inspection and also try to tie in your creative ideas. Most of what is shown on TV is very design heavy or may not be inspected by the local governance. Also, as there are so many hidden nuances in construction every day you want to partner with a home remodeler who isn’t wasting too much time talking about options and increasing the budget before any real work is accomplished. Look for a blend of customer service coupled with experience and work ethic that will begin the process on time.

Patience & Longer Scheduling

As mentioned before the recession caused an effect in construction that still has not completely revived. Currently demand for construction seems to outweigh supply of skilled workers and people. If you are getting involved in residential remodeling expect to see longer scheduling and work with contractors whom are already very, very busy.

Not Full Reliance On Technology

These days you can’t get away from technology and it’s slowly making it’s way into the construction field. It’s getting better yes but still very, very faulty. Many contractors will integrate technology into their day to day (facetime, email, texting, etc.) but they do not use as one might envision in the field. Often even from the design blueprints delivered from the architect the general contractor framing the home will have to make on the spot framing changes to material loads, windows and layout. As much as you may see new technologies arise, don’t expect to see your residential remodeler using it so much in the field, for now technology is more served as preconstruction and as an on-going communication medium.

Green  Awareness

Green building has been a hot topic for a long time. Today you will definitely find your residential remodeler much more knowledgable about building green than past years. Although, residential construction is not as involved in green building as commercial (hospitals, universities, business parks, etc.) design elements and material swifts have caused residential remodelers to look at green building in a new light. So far, not too much change has happened other than more on the paperwork side of things but nonetheless you contractor is getting more accustomed to what is determined to be a green build and what is needed from the city.
