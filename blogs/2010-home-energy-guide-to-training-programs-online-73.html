2010 Home Energy Guide to Training Programs Online

By Leslie Jackson · 26.05.2010

Home Energy magazine’s Annual Training Guide for Home Performance Professionals--published in past years in print and as a downloadable PDF on our Web site--is now perpetual. And searchable. Every two years, we have published a resource of training programs in the United States and Canada, connecting trainers to students. Last year, the Guide went from a list with a short description of each trainer organization and its offerings, to a grid, showing the list of trainers next to a table of various certification types. As the list of trainers grows with demand for certification from professionals and the explosion in the green collar jobs market, that matrix rapidly outgrew our pages, looking like the big board at the New York Stock Exchange.

 

Trainers can register, list their information, purchase optional logo space, and provide their course information, updatable any time. Students visit the site and plug in their search criteria, and receive a custom, printable listing of only the programs they are looking for. No magnifying glass required. Visit the Guide online.
