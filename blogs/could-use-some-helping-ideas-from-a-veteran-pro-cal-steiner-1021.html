Could use some helping ideas from a veteran pro/ Cal Steiner?

By Brian H · 22.06.2017

Insulated roof area and concerns / mobile home
I live in Manitoba Canada and just reinsulated my attic space of my 52 X 14 1977 mobile last Fall(2016'). I blew in Owens Corning fiber fill insulation to maximum level inside attic area to fill/ Max. R value..and with such low pitch there is no soffit ends (which is just a capped dead air space at outside wall/ no real soffit) The home has a newer (2014') high efficiency Lennox furnace with its own fresh air return and insulated original stack /pipe from old furnace is where the intake and exhaust leave thecresidence to the roof. I have a 4/12 pitch roof that has no soffit end venting and the gable ends have a 1' overhang which has numerous 3"x10" screen vents. It has a whirlybird/circulating fan mid-centre south side and 4 other duraflo roof vents installed. After reroofing the residences North side (fall 2016') I pulled out the R14 batted insulation which had some damp spots and molding issues. I also sealed around stacks and tears in vapor barrier at electrical wiring entry/ exit points and bath fan replacement was completed as well from old 1' round straight pipe/ chimney flue style with new flapper vent and new fan in ceiling.. Here starts some issues..
The bath fan replacement,.. it now has a metal 4" diam. collapsible/ accordian pipe that is about 3.5' long snaking from connection point-side mount, to almost directly above to a hook style flapper vent with batted insulation around fan housing and then blown in insulation to roof substrate.. I use the fan in the Winter and humid / moist air, evidently hits the cold metal pipe condenses and drips back down the pipe into fan housing area and has wrecked the ceiling. Answers? Longer run of pipe from fan to raised hook style roof flapper vent ? So to allow slower cooling of moist air before exiting into -10c -40c temps.? Or running a rigid pipe to the closest gable end (14') and flapper/ dryer style vent ?
Secondly my whirlybird/ spinning fan has never frosted up before and roof vents were frosted as well . My blowing in insulation in the entire cavity to a higher r-value has evidently trapped air even with use of four (4)R20 Duraflo vents and a whirlybird isn't enough? My whirlybird area / on top of insulation (after taking off for inspection) was found a lump of ice/snow and it was melting and allowing water drips in numerous spots through ceiling area into residence ..every nail in the roof from shingling was a 3" icicle  Arghh..Answers? Close off whirlybird and install roof vent or a full ridge vent entire length of mobile and leave other / existing vents as well in place to maximize ventilation or install mechanical ventilation at gable ends with blower and flapper ? Open up end wall cap area and extend trusses a foot and putvin soffit venting?

I completed the drywalling ceiling, over the existing 16" panels of original ceiling buffalo board (we call it) starting in bathroom remodel . As I was hoping to alleviate humid air / heated air from getting through/ into attic area to lesson the cause of humidity issues/ evaporation/ condensation buildup? But the ceiling in the Spring had water dripping into the bathroom and wrecked the drywall tape joints and as mentioned earlier the metal accordion style pipe eve night with wrap to roof substrate , dripped back threw fan housing grrr...
Would appreciate advice ASAP
Thank you
