Finding Buried Treasure at The National Healthy Homes Conference (NHHC)

By Allen Rathey · 12.06.2014
The National Healthy Homes Conference (NHHC)—brought to you by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) and Rebuilding Together, and media clout from the HGTV and the DIY Network is a gold mine of information.
 
NHHC’s six program tracks embraced 130-140 educational sessions and workshops featuring experts with dozens of nationally recognized organizations presenting a wealth of information, including actionable steps for:
  • Communities, homebuilders, designers and remodelers
  • Environmental, public health and safety professionals
  • Urban and city planners
  • Federal, state and local health & housing organizations
  • Philanthropic & non-profit organizations
  • Energy & weatherization professionals
  • Educational institutions
 
Take Away Nuggets
 
NHHC’s excellent take-away education values included: 
 
  • Identifying & bridging the gap between housing and health
  • Showcasing trends in healthy homes policy & standards
  • Outlining how healthy homes are for the mainstream, not just the wealthy
  • Exploring research & innovation to create healthier homes, communities
  • Developing new partnerships across disciplines
  • Reducing inequality in healthy housing 
 
Hidden Gems in the Exhibit Hall and Poster Sessions
 
The exhibit hall had scores of booths highlighting diverse healthy home tools and assistance programs, plus 50 poster sessions containing many ‘hidden gems’ for those patient enough to review their content.
