Decorating Bedrooms of All Styles

By Alisson Chan · 06.02.2015
Bedrooms are often the last rooms homeowners decorate because they are the last places guests usually see when visiting a home.  Bedrooms are fun to decorate, but it can be a costly project, and often times might not seem worth all the effort and money when only the homeowners spend time in them.  There are many ways to create a beautiful bedroom on a tight budget, and when all else fails, the trick is to stay simple when it comes to bedrooms.  Here are some suggestions for homeowners looking to spruce up bedrooms in their home, but do not want to spend a ton of money on the project.
 
Choosing the right colors
 
Colors mean everything when it comes to a bedroom.  When decorating a bedroom, there are not many colors that cannot be modernized aside from the obvious colors that are never in style (and you all know what colors I am talking about).  Paint colors and bed linens should always be chosen together so it is certain that they are cohesive.  If worse comes to worse, choose bed linens first and cater paint colors to the linens because it is a lot harder to find linens that match paint than it is to match paint to linens that are already found and purchased.  Also keep in mind that paint can always be changed, so if you are decorating a child’s room and they want a bold color that you know they will not like in the years to come, go ahead and use the paint anyway!
 
Modernizing a bedroom
 
Clean lines are back in style, but they must include a twist of comfort according to She Knows.  Use throw pillows, rugs and soft furniture to give a room a comfortable yet modern feel.  Anything can be modernized with throw pillows or furniture that has the right texture or pattern.  Take notes while walking through décor stores of the patterns that are top of the line, and then take the time to reupholster a chair or footstool with fabrics in those patterns.  You will save much more money refurbishing or reupholstering furniture than if you simply by the newest furniture in stores.
 
Creating a romantic space
 
When creating a romantic master bedroom, do away with frilly décor.  A master bedroom should be warm and sophisticated.  Furniture should be bold, sophisticated colors such as dark wood.  Lighting should set the mood. If you desire to use floral patterns, make sure they are not too girly (even though floral patterns scream girly).  You want your other half to feel comfortable in the room as well, and if it is warm and welcoming, you both will enjoy the space.
 
Adding a personal twist
 
Bedroom décor should reflect the person living in it.  So even though you want to keep up with the latest trends, put a personal spin on them to give a room unique character that reflects the personality of the person who owns the room.
