8 Air Conditioning Tips to Beat the Heat

By Joe Robbins · 01.02.2017

The most scorching summer days can really make your air conditioner work at its toughest. As a result, reducing utility bills, increasing home comfort, and saving energy become a tough challenge for many homeowners. So here are energy-saving tips when air conditioning is a must:

  1. Check the filter.

During the hot summer days, dust, allergens and other specs of dirt circulate around your home. And if the air filter gets clogged, your air conditioner will have to work tougher just to supply the same level of cool air. Hence, check the filter monthly and know when it needs replacement.

  1. Keep it low at night.

During nighttime, you don’t actually need the same level of cool air. Hence, it’s wise to keep your air conditioner lower during bedtime. If your AC unit has a ‘sleep mode’ feature, use it to lower the output on the timer.

  1. Close the vents.

The coolest room in your home is typically the basement. Hence, try to close all vents in the lower part of your home. This way, the cool air will naturally come down and by closing all the vents, the air rises up first and then cools as it comes down. 

  1. Look into the ducts.

Be sure that your ducts are properly insulated in order to keep air sinking in as cool as much as possible. So check where those ducts run through your home and save a lot on your utility bills. Remember- it isn’t anyone’s wish to spend money on half cool air.

  1. Reorder your furniture.

Rearrange all furniture that blocks air conditioning vents to make way for cool air to sink in the room. Surely, you’d want to enjoy that chill for yourself and for your family. Yes, you can use some plastic pieces for your vents to force the cool air in the right path, but the simplest trick is to reshuffle your furniture, even just in the meantime.

  1. Pay attention to lighting.

While it is true that turning the lights off can save energy, paying attention to the amount of lighting the passes through your open windows can also go a long way to reduce utility bills. You might think it neurotic to open and then close those windows daily, but it actually makes a big difference in your energy-saving endeavor.

  1. Use a fan.

Your air conditioner may be doing all the cooling thing, but using a fan won’t hurt anyone. Use fans so the air can circulate around your home with less energy used compared to your air conditioning unit. Just keep a fan as a backup and you’ll thank us later.

  1. Hire an HVAC professional.

More than anyone else, heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) professionals know enough about proper AC maintenance. Your AC unit needs to be inspected, cleaned or tuned up by a professional HVAC contractor before the hot days set in.

Running your air conditioning unit can make the bill skyrocket. Luckily, the abovementioned tips can help your AC unit run better and save more money as the summer days set in.
