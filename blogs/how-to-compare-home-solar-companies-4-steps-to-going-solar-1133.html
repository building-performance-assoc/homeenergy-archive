How to compare Home Solar Companies? 4 Steps to Going Solar

By Kyle Graycar · 08.08.2018

A home solar system is an energy asset. It’s a long-term investment for your eco friendly home. In order to get a high quality solar installation, you must choose a high quality solar provider. To select the best home solar system, it’s important to find a local solar installation company that makes going solar easy and efficient for you.

 

Are you wondering where to start? If you're confused on how to move forward with your home solar project, follow this step-by-step guide to find the right solar provider for you.

 

1.)  List out the available local solar providers:

 

The smartest way to compare solar providers is to create a document that has all the necessary details. Gather all the available information on small, local businesses and national companies that provide service in your state.

 

To start off, type a Google query for 'solar providers near me' and note down the information. Once you have the list of companies, you can start to fill out the other information as discussed in the next steps.

 

You can also use online marketplaces like Pick My Solar to see verified installation companies near your zip code.



2.) Evaluate the Pros and Cons of choosing a Local Installer or a National Company.

 

When comparing the solar providers, try to evaluate the differences between a local installer and a national level company and decide which company fulfill your needs.

 

You have to select a provider which offers:

  • Lower Prices
  • Strong Customer Service
  • Quality Work, Equipment, and Warranties





3.) Financing Options:

A few different ways to finance solar panels include paying cash, taking out a loan, leasing or Power Purchase Agreement (PPA). You’ll want to find out which options each company on your list offers and make note of it.

 

Focus on companies that provide multiple financing methods so you can consult them to make your decision.

 

4.) Ask for a Quote:

Once you narrow down your options to top picks, reach out to them and ask for a Quote. Most companies will want to schedule an appointment to see your property. Technically, this is not required to get accurate prices, so be wary that they may send salespeople rather than engineers.

 

Companies like Pick My Solar will remotely provide detailed system designs, unbiased advice, competitive options from top providers on their marketplace. You can also use their free solar estimates tool to learn more about potential options in your area.
