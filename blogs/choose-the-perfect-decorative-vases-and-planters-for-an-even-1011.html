Choose the perfect Decorative vases and planters for an event!

By SNK Enterprises · 06.06.2017

Decorative vases and planters for an event are now taking the décor idea by storm. With a modern yet classy approach, all the guests in arriving for an event will certainly appreciate the stunning decoration. If you are planning to celebrate an occasion; create a perfect impression of yours by incorporating these décor elements.

How to choose the perfect decorative vases and planters for an event?

When it comes to choosing the perfect decorative piece like vases and planters, there are some imperative factors to keep in mind. Once you are sure about these aspects, choosing and selecting will be easier for you!

Material

There are different types of material available for vases and planters. As per the event, you can try selecting from – metals like brass, aluminum, copper or crystal and glass.

For wedding decoration ideas, metallic vases will look great. For corporate events, glass is a better option and crystal goes well with everything!

Where to place

First, chalk out where you want to place these decorative pieces. Depending on that you will decide on the size. If you want to place it on a table, go for a small one. As for the floor, a longer height will suit better.

Color scheme

The best part about these planters and vases is that they blend well with most of the colors. If you are using bright summer colors, stick to crystal and glass and for darker hues, try metal decorative vases and planters for an event.

Shape

These are available in different shapes, and all look nice. You can try pedestals, urns, trumpet shape, pots, etc. but keep the size in mind as it should not look too big or too small.

Once you are done choosing, you can now place them creatively in different events!

How to use planters and vases creatively?

Is the same concept of putting flowers in vases and planters boring you? Want to try something different? Here are some ideas that can help you to make more creative decoration!

1. Place fruits in the metallic vases

You can use wide vases and place fruits in it! This looks stunning especially as centerpieces on tables. Also, if you want to try something new for your D-day, these centerpieces will work so great for wedding decoration ideas.

2. Put corals or shells in glass planters and vases

Use colorful shells and corals and put it inside glass vases and planters. If you are using planters, you can hang them for a more astounding look!

3. Use candles inside glass vases

Yes, you can do that. Also, you can try filling ½ of the glass vase with color water and add a layer of oil on top; then place floating candle on it. It will instantly increase the charm of the event.

These are few innovative ways in which you can use vases and planters. You can buy them online from authentic sites and get some good discounts too! All these beautiful decorative vases and planters for an event can also be used as home décor! Be a pro at decorating events!
