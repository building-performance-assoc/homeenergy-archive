How to save money and energy with a through-wall fan

By Bernie Pierce · 07.12.2012

In the coming months, many of us will spend our time hibernating indoors away from the cold. The winter season doesn’t have to mean high energy bills and cold rooms, which translates to layers of sweaters and blankets. The ComfortGeni is one solution to winter heating woes as it distributes warm air room to room and level to level. With the ComfortGeni, there is no need to jack up the thermostat to warm or cool rooms. It can balance the temperature in an economical way, and for just pennies a day. The ComfortGeni uses no more electricity than a 14-watt light bulb and costs less than $5 a year to operate.

The ComfortGeni works in two ways. First, it can be easily installed as a through-wall fan to complement fireplaces, wood and pellet stoves. The fan has an automatic temperature sensor that turns off when the desired rooms are heated to a sufficient degree. This “auto-off” feature ensures that energy is not wasted. The product is a great alternative to having a heating system run constantly. It’s a simple device for any DIY-er to install, and the electronics used in the ComfortGeni utilize lead free solder and are designed to be environmentally friendly.  

The ComfortGeni can also be used as a register booster fan to increase output for an existing central HVAC system. The product can be placed in any register, improving air distribution, quality and efficiently distributing heat throughout the home. ComfortGeni can warm rooms that have uneven temperatures 5 to 7 degrees without having to adjust the thermostat. It increases airflow in rooms that need it most. Typically, 56 percent of your utility bill goes toward heating and cooling, but the ComfortGeni can save between 10 to 20 percent on utility bills.

Currently available in almond color, the ComfortGeni model Geni 6xr blends into any décor and fits flush to the floor, wall or ceiling. It retails for $134.95. You can purchase the product from a retailer near you. Retailers interested in carrying the product should contact a distributor in their area.
