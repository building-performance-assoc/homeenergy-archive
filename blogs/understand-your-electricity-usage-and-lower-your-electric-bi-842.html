Understand Your Electricity Usage and Lower Your Electric Bills

By www.chooseenergy.com · 13.06.2016

As summer heats up, there are many measures homeowners can take to reduce their energy consumption and lower their electric bills. First, homeowners should take a few minutes to understand where the majority of their energy usage comes from, then take measures to save on their electric bills.

 

We’ve collected data on the typical breakdown of a household’s energy usage:

  • Space Heating: roughly 40%
  • Electronics, Appliance, and Lighting: 30% - 40%
  • Water Heating: roughly 20%
  • Air Conditioning: 10% or less

 

Now that you know the largest contributors to your home's monthly energy usage, here are some simple measures that can have the greatest results in reducing your overall home energy usage and monthly electric bill:

  • Cut down on space heating costs by lowering your thermostat during colder weather months and raising your thermostat during warmer weather months. One degree of temperature adjustment translates into about 1% of monthly bill savings! Space heating varies by location and has generally reduced in recent years because of more efficient home heating equipment and insulation.
  • Reduce your appliance-related energy costs by using EnergyStar labeled appliances, which are government-certified to meet particular energy saving requirements.
  • Reduce electronics-related energy costs by unplugging them when they are not in use. Electronics still consume some electricity even when they are turned off. Some of the biggest culprits are computers, video game consoles, and stereo components. An easy way to accomplish this goal is to plug multiple electronics into a power strip with a surge protector and off switch that you can turn off when they are not in use.
  • Reduce lighting costs by switching from incandescent to fluorescent and halogen bulbs, which produce the same amount of light but are far more energy-efficient. Also, turn off unused lights throughout the day.

By taking these simple measures you can ensure that your efforts to reduce your home's energy consumption have the highest impact.
