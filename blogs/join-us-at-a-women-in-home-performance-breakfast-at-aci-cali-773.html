Join Us at a Women in Home Performance Breakfast at ACI California

By Amy Beley · 01.11.2015
It's been exciting to read the stories of these great women in our industry in The Real Women of Home Performance blog series. While we certainly need more women and diversity in general in our industry, I'm energized at what many women in our industry are doing to improve homes, save energy, and make happy homeowners.
 
To keep the momentum going, I'd like to invite you to join me at breakfast in Sacramento at ACI California. This is not an exclusive breakfast—all are welcome! I want to hear from you about what you'd like to see the Women in Home Performance initiative do for the industry in the coming year. It will be a great opportunity to network with your counterparts interested in supporting women in the industry. We are jump starting this conference season with a Women in Home Performance breakfast. Come hang out with me in Sacramento at 7:30am on Tuesday, November 10.
 
Hope to see you there! And if not there, at ACI National in Austin next April. Stay tuned!
 
 
Amy Beley is a Senior Manager at Build It Green.
