Best time to sell your house

By Sarah Stilo · 10.08.2018

 

Do You Know the Best Time to Sell in Los Angeles?

 

If you’re ready to pack up the van and move out of your home in the city of Angels, then you most likely want to sell your house fast and for the most cash you can. Who wouldn’t, right?

 

There’s actually hard numbers behind the best time to sell in Los Angeles: data aggregation from the past four years reveals significant trends in the Los Angeles real estate market. This data suggests that if you list and close on your home sale at a specific time of year, you can actually make more money. If you have the scheduling flexibility and don’t need capital immediately, then you can game the real estate market by timing your sale when homes are the most in demand.

 

Get ready to master the Los Angeles real estate market. Here’s the best time to sell a house in LA.

 

Months Where You Can Make the Most Money Selling in LA

 

If you’ve ever heard that Spring is the best time to sell your house, it’s actually true for Los Angeles. From 2014 - 2017, May to August was the best time to sell. May was the best time to list a home, as home sales that closed in July sold for 4.75% higher than the national average.

 

Winter was not a good time to sell in Los Angeles overall. A good rule of thumb is that when the holiday lights go up the “for sale” signs go down! Homes sold November through April went for less than the national average. January was, overall, the worst time to sell a house. Homes sold in January went for 5.26% lower than the national average. Yikes.

 

2016 was a hot year for the Los Angeles real estate market. February through September was a great time to sell, with homes going for more than the national average every month. Homes sold in February sold 4.62% over the national average, which is surprising given the trend towards Spring home sales the other three years.

 

This change could be based on a multitude of different factors like better weather, changes in a  neighborhood, an increase or decrease of jobs, etc.

 

Months Where You Can Sell Your Home the Fastest in LA

 

If you don’t want to deal with months of keeping your home as clean as a five star hotel, entertaining different offers, and leaving at odd hours for showings, then you’ll want to pick the time of year where you can sell your home the fastest.

 

Overall from 2014 - 2017, April through October was the best time to sell your house fast. April was the best time to list your home for a June close date. Homes that closed in June sold 5 days faster than the national average.

 

January was by far the worst month to sell a home. Homes sold in January took anywhere from 14 to 18 days longer to sell than average.

 

The Key to a Faster, Stronger Home Sale in Los Angeles

 

The best way you can master your LA home sale is to combine this data with the help of a top real estate agent.

 

As the Los Angeles market continues to grow rapidly in 2018, an experienced real estate agent on the ground in your Los Angeles neighborhood will be able to best predict why certain months are better to sell than others.

 

There are also tips to selling your home like staging it, deep cleaning it, and creating amazing curb appeal that will improve your chances of a high value sale even more.

 

Overall, the best time to sell your home in Los Angeles is affected by your specific neighborhood, your timeline, the condition of your house, and the demand of the Los Angeles market. A real estate agent is your best resource to make the most out of your home sale.
