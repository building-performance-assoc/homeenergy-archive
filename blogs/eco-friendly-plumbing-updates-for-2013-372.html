Eco-Friendly Plumbing Updates for 2013

By Rachel Kopp · 15.01.2013

Eco-friendly by definition means: Earth friendly. Listed below are ways in which water products can help make a home and business more efficient in water use and being earth friendly.

Water-Saving Plumbing Updates 

1. WaterSense Program: A partnership with the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) offering an easy way to use less water. 

2. Tankless water heater: Also known as Instantaneous or Demand Water Heaters. When selecting this type of water heater take these things into consideration: a. fuel type, b. location, size, demand, c. application. There's electric and gas-fired.

3. Showerheads: Each household uses approximately 30 gallons of water a day taking showers. Using WaterSense Showerheads can save about 2,900 gallons of water a day.

4. Faucets: This is another area where a lot of water used in the home. WaterSense faucets can reduce 30+ percent water flow and still perform well.

5. Toilets: This is the most used water area in the home with 30 percent of a home's use. Using WaterSense toilets will save up to 11 gallons per toilet in a home.

Water-Saving Best Practices

Really save water within the home without breaking the bank. An example, you can have the shower setup with a regulator that will reduce the flow of water, consequently saving a lot of money in the end. Likewise, taking a shower instead of a bath. The bathtub holds more water than using a low-flow shower head. Here are some other tips on saving water:

  • Instead of letting the water run in the sink, fill the sink partially and use to wash food and rinse stuff.
  • Instead of letting the water run when washing your hands, shaving or brushing your teeth, turn off the water. Once finished, use short bursts to rinse mouth and toothbrush as well as washing/rinsing hands.
  • Fix any leaky fixtures.
  • Use aerators on the sink fixtures.
  • Only wash laundry when there is a full load
  • Use a composting system instead of using the garbage disposal in the kitchen sink

Do It Yourself Tricks

With a quick internet search one can find many do it yourself instructions for water efficient products to change out existing old plumbing fixtures. Some of those include:

  • Changing a shower head.
  • Changing sink fixtures.
  • Replacing old commodes.

Create a More Efficient, Eco-Friendly Home

Reviews for eco-friendly plumbing show that thousands of dollars can be saved in a year by switching from the old plumbing fixtures because the old fixtures can be leaky, or sometimes the flapper in the toilet sticks allowing water to run.The tankless water heaters heat on demand so there is no water storage. Everyone likes to save money. Ask your local building contractor and their industry groups if they're eco-friendly and ask what they can do to help current owners for ways to implement eco-friendly plumbing in your home or business.
