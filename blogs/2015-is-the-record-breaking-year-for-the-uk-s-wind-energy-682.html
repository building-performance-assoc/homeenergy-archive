2015 Is the Record Breaking Year For the UK's Wind Energy

By Anja Duric · 15.04.2015

Renewable energy is our future and is also a way to secure energy sources, stabilize costs and develope more jobs and community industry.  We still have long way to go before the implementation and usage of renewable energy is large enough to cover our energy needs and significantly reduce carbon emissions.

2014 was an exceptional year for wind energy, but what about 2015?Recent statistics are showing an annual market growth of 44% and record installations of more than 51 gigawatts of wind energy. Results obtained from a study compiled by the Global Wind Energy Council and GreenMatch shows that China will continue their lead with 45% of the total annual market, Africa and Latin America will continue to emerge, while Europe will face a slight downturn.

The UK is the current leader with 55% of all installations, leaving behind Denmark with 15,8%  and Germany with 13%. Belgium is fourth on the list, followed by the Netherlands with 3,1%, Sweden with 2,6% and Finland with 0,3. 

As the windiest country in Europe, the Uk could power itself several time over using only wind energy. At the present the UK is the leading country with 55,9% of all installed offshore wind capacity in Europe. The UK is exploiting its excellent wind resources to keep the right track in securing 15% of its total energy comes from renewable sources by 2020. Moreover, the UK reached its new monthly record, with 14% of all UK's electricity generated by wind power, beating the previous record of 13% set in 2013.

Due to the continued uncertainties related to changes in the financial regime and the upcoming general elections, the investment levels in wind energy will most likely struggle during 2015. On the positive side, the offshore wind manufacturing facilities constructed by Siemens and MHI Vestas Offshore Wind in the UK will most likely be ready in the course of 2015. Additionaly, there is a hope that the positive outcome of upcoming election will bring stability and support of the growing sector of green energy.
