How to Keep Track of the Texas Electric Grid

By Zonesty.com · 22.09.2015

Well into September, the warm weather in Texas can cause peak electricity demands that put a serious strain on the ERCOT grid.   Already this summer on August 10, Texas hit a new record power demand of 69.8 gigawatts (GW).  Over the last few summers, brown outs caused by disproportionate supply and demand have been a serious concern for consumers and energy officials.

Demand response programs have helped to curb the peak demands that cause the grid to overload, but the need for more power production has been undeniable.  Since last summer, ERCOT has added 6 GW to the grid’s generating capacity to help ease the problem.  This has bumped the electricity reserve margin up to 16.24 percent, which is 1.26 percent higher than last year.

Understanding how the grid is performing at any given time can help you avoid using electricity during peak periods and know when a brown out or black out might occur.  For households with children, senior citizens or individuals with health concerns having a heads up can help you avoid serious problems.

ERCOT Grid Information

ERCOT has taken measures to ensure every Texas resident has access to grid information.  Through their website you can get current conditions, load information, outage reports and trend analysis.  Three features that provide the biggest benefit are:

  • The real-time system conditions report is among the most useful information because this will tell you the current demand and the system power capacity.
  • The load capacity report is another useful tool for seeing the load forecast for a day compared to the actual load.  A graph charts load hour-by-hour and also provides forecasts for the following day.
  • You can check to see if there is a planned, forced or maintenance outage scheduled for your area with the hourly resource outage capacity report.

The ERCOT Mobile App

In addition to real-time reports on the website, ERCOT has also developed a free mobile app for consumers.  It provides all the information you need to track the grid including:

  • Real-time system conditions.
  • Wholesale prices from transmission and distribution utilities.
  • Fuels used to generate electricity.
  • Push notifications when demand peaks and energy needs to be conserved.
  • Ability to share notifications through social media.

This useful tool is worth downloading for the notifications alone so there is no need to manually check the current conditions.  The ERCOT app is now available for both Apple and Android devices.
