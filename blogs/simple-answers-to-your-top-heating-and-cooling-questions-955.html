Simple Answers to Your Top Heating and Cooling Questions

By Cary Teller · 01.02.2017

When it comes to your HVAC system, there are probably many questions on your mind. This guide will help you answer the HVAC questions.

Why Does the HVAC System Need Regular Maintenance?

Your heating and air conditioning system should be checked and receive a tune-up twice a year. Each heating and cooling season call an HVAC company to send a technician to check all the motors, hoses and electrical parts of the system.

When you perform regular maintenance, you're preventing large problems from impacting the unit. They can catch small problems before they turn into a problem that can cost thousands of dollars for a new unit.

When Should the Filters Be Changed?

During your maintenance call with a technician, he or she will replace the air filters, but this should be done more often than twice a year. All the dirt and dust in the air will be filtered through the system. When air filters get clogged, that dust can get blown back into the home. It'll also stop the system from working properly.

Imagine if you had a clogged filter in your car, it wouldn't allow air to get into the engine, which would cause the engine to seize. The same is possible with your HVAC unit. Every month, remove the filter from the unit and clean or replace it.

What Should I Know Before Purchasing an Air Conditioning System?

You'll want to figure out what system has the best overall rating for efficiency. Air conditioning systems are rated from 13 to 18 SEER, which is the seasonal energy efficiency ratio. A furnace has AFUE, which is Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency and range from 80 percent to 90 percent. The higher the efficiency of the unit, the less it'll cost in utility bills.

The sound of your unit can have an impact on the quiet enjoyment of your home. You don't want to have a system that won't allow you to have a nice conversation in your backyard. Bel ratings will let you know what will be comfortable as a sound level. They range from 6.8 bels to 9 bels. Make sure you're checking the rating before purchase.

Before purchasing a system, make sure you understand the warranty. It should come with a manufacturer's warranty as well as a service warranty with the HVAC company. Find out what the HVAC company will do if there's a problem. It's best to know their policies up front.

What is a Split System?

A split air conditioning system is one that has an interior unit as well as a piece that sits outside the home. The indoor part of the system includes the evaporator coil while the outside has the condenser. It's important when you are replacing one of the parts, you replace them both to ensure you're getting a working system that will be like it's brand new.

Do I Need a Programmable Thermostat?

A programmable thermostat will allow you to leave the heating or cooling low while everyone is at work or school. Before everyone gets home, the heating or cooling can be raised to comfortable levels. According to Energy.gov, you can save almost 10 percent on utility bills with this simple thermostat change.

One thing to consider before having the thermostat installed is placement. You want to make sure that it's placed away from direct sunlight as well as windows and drafty areas. When it's reading the temperature of the home, it could get inaccurate readings that will cause the heat or cooling to cycle unnecessarily. That won't save you money on your energy bills.

Why is it Important to Have the Right Size Unit for Air Conditioning?

When you have a system with too much capacity for the home, it'll constantly cool the home quickly, turn itself off and have to turn back on again. When it's over capacity for the size of the home it actually becomes less efficient at cooling. Running in short cycles means that it's cooling then allowing the home to grow warm again where the right capacity will work efficiently and consistently to lower the temperature.

It can be tough to judge the needs of the unit without knowing the size of the home's square footage. A technician can let you know what size unit you need for the square footage of the home. Normally, one ton of air conditioning unit can cool 300 to 800 square feet.
