Political Instability in the Gulf and Price Hike of Crude Oil to Spur Synthetic Fuels Market Development

By Divyaprasad Pande · 02.07.2019

Synthetic fuels are derived from a synthetic gas that is a mixture of hydrogen and carbon monoxide. Synthetic fuel is in a liquid form and is not obtained from the natural crude oil. Such fuels are produced using renewable energy. Production of synthetic fuel involves production of hydrogen from water. In the next step, carbon is added to the derived hydrogen to get liquid fuel. The carbon used for process can be derived either from industrial process or can be captured from air using carbon filters. Finally, the combination of hydrogen and carbon dioxide results in the creation of synthetic fuels. Thus, any fuel derived from hydrogen synthesis can be termed as synthetic fuels.

The global synthetic fuels market is expected to grow at a rapid pace in the coming years. Some of the key drivers are listed below:

 

  • Rise in crude oil prices: One of the primary reasons for the growth of the global synthetic fuels market is the rise in the prices of the natural and crude oil on the international market. Governments that solely rely on importing crude oil are severely affected by this price hike. They are looking for an alternative energy source, which creates a huge opportunity for the development of the synthetic fuel market.

 

  • Clean energy and better efficiency: As mentioned before, synthetic fuels are derived from renewable source of energy. Additionally, synthetic fuels burn more efficiently than fossil fuels. This also works in favor to spur the growth of synthetic fuels market.

 

  • Political instability: Another key factor driving the growth of the global synthetic fuels market is the political instability in the gulf and other oil-producing countries. Such instability creates constant fluctuations in the prices of crude oil, which in turn hamper the economy of importing countries.
