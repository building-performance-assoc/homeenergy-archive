Protect Your Home with Concrete Floors

By Sarah Smith · 27.08.2014

When most people think of concrete flooring, they think of cold and industrial areas; in the past, people never bothered to think of putting concrete in their homes. Today, polished concrete has started to change many people's minds. This type of concrete can take on any color or sheen; combine these color options with its many other benefits, and you have a product that blows away most skeptics.
 
Once people see how beautiful finished concrete looks, they understand why others are so eager to rent out a concrete polisher for their own homes. Consider the following benefits of concrete before installing a new floor:
 
Durable
 
Concrete is tough and resilient. That is exactly why it is used in warehouses where heavy equipment like cars, forklifts and crates consistently put pressure on the ground. If concrete is strong enough to stand up to heavy machinery, then it is very unlikely that you can do anything to damage it. That means you can drop stuff, jump around and move tables over your home's concrete without worrying about it.
 
High heels, pet claws and children can damage other types of flooring; with concrete, damage is almost unheard of. It's possible to chip concrete, but you pretty much have to take a hammer and chisel to it; basically, it never happens.
 
Longevity
 
Polished concrete that is properly taken care of can last for hundreds of years. When you install a quality concrete floor, it will probably be around longer than your actual home is. Homes with concrete look rustic and aged; it can give your home a real classic feel.
 
A long-lasting concrete floor can save homeowners a boatload of money on repairs and upgrades. Once you install concrete, you never have to change it due to damage or aging. 
 
Easy To Take Care Of
 
Once you use a concrete polisher on your floor, there isn't much maintenance that needs to be performed. The floor will look great if you seal or wax it every nine months or so; it really depends on the amount of traffic that you get through your home. If you like to keep your floor clean, then use soapy water with a mop on it every week or so; this should be enough to keep it spotless.
 
Concrete flooring is an inexpensive investment that lasts forever. When you choose concrete, you choose flooring that is strong, durable and that looks great in your home. Tons of people are making the switch to concrete; it is just too good to pass up.
