Home Energy Magazine Features 30 Home Performance Leaders Under 30 to Celebrate 30th Year in Print

By Macie Melendez · 04.09.2014

The September/October 2014 issue of Home Energy marks the magazine’s 30th year in print. To celebrate that milestone, an industry-wide call for nominations found noteworthy professionals in home performance who were 30 years of age or younger. The feature article introduces the young people who the industry can count on to lead the home performance industry for the next 30 years. 

Home Energy’s associate editor, Macie Melendez, compiled the nominations and narrowed them down using criteria that included years of experience, career milestones, number of completed projects, and overall ambition.

“The list of individuals is both wonderful and inspiring,” says Home Energy editor Jim Gunshinan, who has been working for the magazine for nearly 14 years.

The “30 Under 30” cover article includes a photo, background information, and a short interview with each featured professional. Among the interview questions asked was  “What does the industry need to stay relevant for the next 30 years?”

Some of the responses include:

“A relentless pursuit of innovation.”

–Gavin Hastings, Tierra Resource Consultants, LLC

“Greater education.”

–Andrew Corral, Elysian Energy

“A continued push to make energy efficiency cost-effective.”

–Dustin Pohlman, Energize Engineering

The article can be read in print, online, and in the digital replica edition of Home Energy at HomeEnergy.org.      

“I am so happy that these bright, passionate, young people will be carrying on the work we do together for another generation,” says Gunshinan.
