Building to code is a waste of money, GUARANTEED

By Anna Svensson · 26.02.2016

Repurposed from Better Buildings: Better Business Conference blog post on February 1, 2016 | by conference presenter Steve Romme, Partner, eFree Advisors, LLC

I bet you have had more than one person tell you as a builder that you should be building better, more efficient new homes. Yet in real life, your clients don’t want to spend more on energy savings. They want to spend the money they have on upgraded cabinetry, counters, flooring and technology. Besides, the Uniform Dwelling Code [Editor's note: Wisconsin's statewide building code for one- and two-family dwellings built since June 1, 1980, enforced in all Wisconsin municipalities] already has energy efficiency minimums that ensure builders are providing energy efficient new homes.

After analyzing, optimizing and tracking the costs and energy bills of many new homes, I know building to code will result in higher costs for the homeowners. That’s right, building to UDC minimums is a waste of money and will always cost the homeowners more than if the home were optimized. What’s more, is that an optimized home not only can have a lower cost of ownership, it can also be safer, healthier and more comfortable.

So why aren’t more homes optimized?

Optimizing a new home from drawings is complex and takes a high degree of expertise and experience. It involves not just estimating the energy consumption of the home and its components, but also understanding different construction methods, mechanical systems, component costs and energy costs. In order to optimize a new home thousands of combinations need to be considered, which seems impossibly complex and expensive. So instead of attempting to optimize, most builders fall back to UDC, or even worse they do what their sub-contractors suggest makes the most sense.

Optimization is here and it works!

At eFree Advisors, we use our experience and a computer program to compare the most important options and recommend the best combination that results in a lower cost of homeownership as defined by the mortgage payment plus energy bill. We generally reduce energy bills by about 40 percent and increase build costs by about 2-4 percent, which always results in lower combined monthly bills. Better yet, the energy savings can add up quickly over the years with increasing energy costs. Considering the effect of 5 percent energy inflation, many homeowners will SAVE OVER $40,000 BY NOT BUILDING TO CODE! If energy inflation is higher than 5 percent per year, their savings get even better. As an interesting side note, we also provide better indoor air quality and comfort when optimizing a home. More on those subjects in another blog…

Where do you start?

So optimizing a new home is worth it, but where do you start? Start by asking yourself and your clients if you are open to change. Are you willing to change the way you build and buy different equipment if it results in a better, more efficient new home? Are your clients willing to shift money to reduce their final cost of ownership? These seem like simple questions, but in my experience most builders will continue to build like they have always built and most new home buyers will buy what they like and figure out how to pay for it later. If you are ready to build better, more cost effective new homes, the market is clearly waiting.

Builders that we have worked with over the past few years have differentiated themselves in their markets by offering the great craftsmanship they have become known for over the years, with a new found, technologically driven optimization process that enhances home ownership. New home buyers can now get it all; a new home that is beautiful, more comfortable, more energy efficient and more affordable through the use of optimization. Don’t believe it? Well, what if I said we now guarantee it?

Put your money where your mouth is

That’s right, after a couple of years of development and optimization of over 50 new homes, eFree Advisors is now GUARANTEEING TO LOWER THE COST OF OWNERSHIP BY NOT BUILDING TO CODE and GUARANTEEING the ACTUAL HEATING AND COOLING BILLS for FIVE YEARS!

We believe strongly that every new home can be built better and for less. And we may be the first ever to put our money where our mouth is by guaranteeing to show every builder and every new homeowner that it can be done on their project.
