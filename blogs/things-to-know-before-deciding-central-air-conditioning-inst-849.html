Things to Know Before Deciding Central Air Conditioning Installation in Brooklyn

By Angelina Paul · 30.06.2016

Summers in Manhattan and Brooklyn can be killing, so before the sun starts scorching, consider getting a central air conditioner installed in your home. By installing a central AC you can make sure that you stay comfortable inside your home, no matter how much the temperature rises outside.

But before you think of central air conditioning installation in Manhattan, there are certain things that you must know. If you are thinking about upgrading your window or split AC systems with the latest and advanced central air conditioner, here are things that you must know:

Get the Right Size

Just as in case of window and split ACs, it is necessary that you choose a central AC with optimal cooling capacity. This means you have to get the tonnage right. A central AC which is larger than needed would cool quickly, but the dehumidification will not be satisfactory; whereas a smaller central AC would mean faster wearing of the machine and increased electricity bills.  If you don’t have an idea about what tonnage you need for your home, you can ask the professionals for a heat-load calculation for your house.

Existing Ducts Work Well

Many people have the perception that new ducts need to be created for new AC systems, but that is just not the case. if you already have ducts on the walls and the floors, they are good enough for the new AC systems. If a contractor tells you need new ducts, look for another contractor. However, if the ducts are leaky, then you might require new ones. So ask the installation professionals to check the existing ducts before installing the new air conditioner.

Wait Till the Winter

If you have a very tight budget, then you might consider waiting till the winter. Since the demand for air conditioners is very less during the winter, the dealers are ready to give heavy discounts to the customers. The installation charges might also be lower during the winter. But that means you will have to sweat day and night during the summer season. So, decide which options suits you better.

Hire the Right Installation Professionals

Most air conditioner manufacturers state that the AC systems must be installed according to the Local & National Mechanical Safety Codes. Thus you need to hire experienced, licensed and certified professionals for central air conditioning installation in Brooklyn. Experienced AC installation experts are aware of all the safety codes and make sure the ACs are installed efficiently to ensure better cooling and longer life. Since you will be investing a lot of money to get the new central air conditioning system in place, you need to make sure it is installed only by the best technicians (to avoid nightmares). Interview many contractors, compare rates and services and then zero in on the one who satisfies all your requirements.
 
Compare your choices and buy the right central air conditioner from the right dealer and get it installed by the right contractor. By following this advice you can have peace of mind and stay cool all through the summer!
