What Is the Average Payback Period of 5kW Solar System?

By Gaurav Keshri · 23.12.2017

Although 5kW solar panel offers a number of environmental and other advantages, the main reason due to which customers go solar is because of the savings. Take a look:

  • Whether you make an investment in your solar PV system with cash, leases or low-interest loans, solar panels reduce your monthly energy bills immediately from day 1.
  • With a large enough solar system installation in your home, the chances of getting $0 utility bills are high. Even some solar customers have received negative electricity bills.

Because of the above reasons, 5kW solar is an investment and not a traditional expense.

But here the question arises – what is the average payback period for a solar PV installation?

5kW solar Average Payback Periods

The average payback periods depends on different multiple factors, that includes:

  • The cost of grid electricity in your location. The payback period is comparatively shorter in markets with expensive utility rates.
  • The amount of clean energy your solar power produces. The output generated by solar depends the amount of direct sunlight your system receives properly and of course the quality of your installation.
  • The total cost of 5kW solar System including all its incentives like tax credits, net metering, and rebates (vary by region).

How you choose to invest in your solar installation. Renting solar panel system offers the most immediate savings. This is because; you receive a brand new installation free of cost and thus continue to enjoy years of lower electricity bills. But when you own your own solar system, your monthly saving will ultimately exceed the upfront installation cost.

No two payback periods are exactly the same because each installation and customer is different.

But we can still express in averages.

And here’s why:

The average payback period of your 5kW solar system becomes shorter as grid electricity rates continue to rise with time and the Return on Investment (ROI) of your solar system becomes higher.

No matter what your local electricity company chooses to charge you in the coming future, you will get the sunlight forever for free.

You enjoy free electricity for the remaining lifetime of your solar panel installation after reaching the average payback period. (Most of the solar panel comes with a warranty of 25-years, but many solar PV installations often last 40 years or longer).

Conclusion

Overall solar is a low-risk investment that offers free clean electricity, lower monthly bills, a smaller carbon footprint, and unparalleled environmental and financial returns.
