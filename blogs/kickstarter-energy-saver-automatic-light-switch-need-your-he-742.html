KickStarter 'Energy saver automatic light switch' need your help

By Eli Blank · 11.09.2015

 EcoFlipper - 'Energy saver automatic light switch' - Now live on KickStarter

Energy waste exist everywhere and contributes to global warming and affects the environment. There are so many examples where lights are kept ON for hours and days even when no one is present. As environment sensitive individuals, we felt that we should do something about this, so we came up with this inexpensive and simple solution called EcoFlipper (Patent Pending). Over a few months, we designed and tested it and even manufactured several prototypes. Now that it is fully functional, we want to get it into production.

EcoFlipper operates by two AA batteries and installed over a standard US Toggle Light Switch. EcoFlipper will automatically return the light switch to an off position, after the predetermined period with no human presence, expires, saving otherwise wasted energy. The key attributes of our device is its ability to be installed over an existing toggle light switch within a minute and without any alteration to the existing switch - eliminating the need for an electrician. Programmable to a set of options to meet any commercial and residential needs . Mimics the look and feel of existing switch, Economical solution with desirable ROI, that continues to save energy. 

We hope you will find a few minutes to review our presentation and join us to make the world more energy efficient and environmentally conscious.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide an eco friendly solution that reduces substantial electricity waste by getting our inexpensive device installed in every room where lights is kept ON for hours while no one is in the room.
