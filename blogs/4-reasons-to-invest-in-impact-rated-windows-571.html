4 Reasons to Invest in Impact Rated Windows

By Jason Fowler · 19.05.2014

If you live somewhere inland, one of your top considerations when choosing new windows for your home is probably efficient insulation. However, when you live close to the coast in states like South Carolina and Florida, you’ll most likely have two top considerations: insulation… and protection against hurricanes. You’ll want windows that are going to help keep your home energy efficient, but that can also hold up against hurricane-force winds and flying debris.

The solution for many coastal homeowners is to invest in impact rated windows. These windows are designed to do exactly what the name implies—stand up against the type of heavy impacts that coastal storms can bring. In some regions that have been designated as High Velocity Hurricane Zones, homebuilders are actually required to install impact rated windows.

If you aren’t in a High Velocity Hurricane Zone but still live along the coast, impact rated windows may still be a good choice for your house. Here are a few reasons to consider them in your remodel or new home construction.

Protect Your Home from High-Speed Debris

Impact rated windows undergo rigorous testing to ensure that they can stand up against the types of debris you might encounter in a hurricane. The standard test is to fire a 9 pound 2x4 at 34 mph so that it hits the window end on. When you invest in impact rated windows, you can rest assured that your windows will hold up even in a fierce storm, and you and your family will be safe inside.

Keep Your Home Secure

Speaking of safety, impact rated windows act as a great security measure against break-ins and thefts. If a 9 pound wood beam traveling at 34 mph can’t break your windows, a burglar will certainly have a hard time getting in. Impact window manufacturers stress that they can’t guarantee that any window will be completely impervious to break-ins, but impact-rated windows will at the very least slow down an intruder, giving you or your alarm system enough time to contact law enforcement.

Avoid Fading Furniture

You might love letting the sunlight into your home, but you probably don’t like it when your furniture, curtains, and carpets fade as a result. Impact rated windows actually provide up to 99% UV protection, which means that natural light can still get through them, but your home doesn’t have to suffer discoloration.  

Reduce Home Energy Use

We obviously couldn’t write an article for Home Energy without talking about the effect impact rated windows have on your home energy usage. Laminated impact rated windows act as great insulators because of their thickness, reducing your need to heat your home in the winter and cool it in the summer. Some brands, like Eco Window Systems, even use a resin interlayer between their two glass panes for strong binding and insulation. The interlayer reduces the thermal conduction of the glass, which helps keep your home from getting too hot or cold.

If you’re interested in installing impact rated windows, talk to a local environmentally-friendly builder about your options. Look for building contractors who have an Energy Star partner label, indicating that the builder is committed to protecting the natural environment and using the impact rated windows that will be best for your home.
