Celebrate Summer by Going Green!

By GreenTouch Pest Solutions · 21.06.2017

Summer is a time for growing, and a time where many people clean out the remaining junk and clutter that they missed during the spring. However, decluttering isn’t the only way you can kick off summer in your home this year. Why not take it up a notch and spend this summer going green?

 10 Ways to Go Green at Home

It doesn’t have to be expensive or difficult to make some eco-friendly changes and additions to your home — it can be easy, hassle-free, and inexpensive. Check out GreenTouch Pest Solutions’ top 10 ways to go green at home this year!

 1) Purge Your Home of Paper
Although it can be convenient, some paper products in your home are easily wasted and are bad for the environment. One easy way to go green at home this summer is to limit your use of extra paper products. Instead of using paper plates and paper cups, use reusable ones. Cut down on paper towels by using rags for your cleaning needs and go electronic when it comes to paying bills. Every little bit of paper saved helps...and the trees will thank you!

 2) Try Reusable Shopping Bags
If you’re like most homeowners, you have tons of those plastic grocery bags, and the mountain just keeps getting bigger with every trip to the store! These bags are bad for the environment and they create clutter in your home. Rather than continuing to collect unnecessary plastic bags in your pantry, invest in some high-quality reusable shopping bags. Not only will you use fewer disposable bags, but the sturdier reusable ones will stand up better in your car, meaning fewer accidents in your trunk!

 3) Get Some Indoor Plants

Having a small plant indoors doesn’t just look green — it is green! There are many benefits from having a small plant indoors, including cleaner air and a relaxing atmosphere. You can even get your kids in on the action by teaching them about taking care of plants...a win-win!

 4) Get a Programmable Thermostat
Everyone has a preferred temperature in their home, but what about when you’re not around? If you leave your heat and air conditioning on while you are away from the house, you’re wasting energy heating and cooling an empty home! To go green and save some money while you’re at it, turn your heat and AC off or down when you leave the house. To make it even easier, invest in a programmable thermostat so all the work is done for you.  

5) Switch to Green Cleaning Products
You might not think about it, but the products you use to clean your home are full of chemicals that can be hazardous to your health. That spray you use on your countertops might be getting into the food you eat when you prepare it on that same surface. One quick and easy way to go green at home is to switch your chemical cleaning products for plant-based and eco-friendly ones. Not only are these safer for your family and pets, but they work just as well and you can feel good knowing you’re not spraying chemicals all around your home.

 6) Make Recycling Convenient

Recycling is one of the greenest things you can do in your home, but it can be hard to follow through with this effort if it’s not convenient. If you want to ramp up your home’s recycling efforts, make it as easy as possible to recycle your items. Place recycle bins in a convenient and easy-to-access place so you don’t end up just throwing things in the trash. If your neighborhood doesn’t offer recyclable pickup, you can find your local recycling center and make a trip one day per week. When you make recycling items in your home convenient, it’s easy to go green!

 7) Switch Your Lights for LED Bulbs
The lights in your home use a lot of energy, especially if you leave them on a lot. However, you can keep your lights on and still save on your energy bill! Swap out your regular light bulbs for LED ones, which are brighter and last much longer than traditional light bulbs. By investing in high-quality LED lights, you can enjoy energy savings without having to be in the dark!

 8) Ditch Plastic Water Bottles
One of the most wasteful inventions is the plastic water bottle. You can help reduce the number of water bottles that are in landfills, and the amount of money you waste each year buying them, by opting to use reusable water bottles in your home instead. Find a quality water bottle that fits your style, and use it for all your hydration needs instead of reaching for a disposable one. You’ll save money and the environment at the same time.

 9) Save Water When You Can

Water is one of our most precious resources, and saving water is one of the best ways to go green at home. Start with little things, like taking shorter showers and turning the faucet off while you brush your teeth. You can also install low-flow toilets and consider drought-resistant landscaping around your home to go the extra mile. With a few small changes to your water consumption, you can save water and a chunk of change on your water bill every month.

 10) Use Energy-Efficient Appliances

If you’re in the market for new appliances, it wouldn’t hurt to look at switching your old ones for new energy-efficient ones. Whether you are looking for an efficient refrigerator or dishwasher, or are looking for new laundry machines, choosing energy efficient models can make a big impact on your energy and water consumption year after year.

 Going green doesn’t have to be difficult or expenses. Try one of these 10 ways to go green and work your way through the list. Soon, you will have a green, eco-friendly, energy efficient home that you can be proud of!
