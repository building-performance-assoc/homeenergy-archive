Fashion Model Cracks The Whip On Water Waste

By Kat Higgins · 12.06.2012

Many people think that a home for a fashion model is a ravenous one filled with eco-enemy fur coats, cocaine & 10.6oz cans of hair spray… but Mexican fashion model Carla Houston begs to differ.  Her home is the environment; the same planet Earth we (Eco-Friendly people) try to manicure every day.  In her latest online spot called "Water" Carla cracks the whip on water waste as she teams up with director Jeffrey Karoff of The Artists Company and Model Environment (ME), an organization of fashion models bringing awareness to environmental and social issues.  Carla makes it clear from her personal life that her care for the environment is authentic; that she's not just a pretty face looking for some extra air time on this spot.  Check out her blog to see what she is up to in figuring out how to make this environment a better place.  Pick up some trash and help this natural beauty make the planet smile! ; )
