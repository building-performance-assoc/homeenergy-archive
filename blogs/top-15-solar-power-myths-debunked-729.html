Top 15 Solar Power Myths Debunked

By SolarLinker · 23.07.2015

Are These 15 Solar Myths Costing You Money? - SolarLinker

 

1. Solar power is weak

Enough sunshine hits the Earth every hour to cover the world's electricity needs for an entire year. A 4kWp solar PV system in the UK is enough to cut your electricity bills by 50%!

2. Going solar is expensive

The price of solar has been dramatically reducing over time and is now 100 times cheaper than it was in the 1970s. A 4kWp system nowadays costs just £6,500, including installation!

3. Solar is not a wise investment

On average, it takes 9 years for solar panels to pay for themselves. Solar has an annualised return on investment of 10.2%, better than most pension funds and property investments!

4. It's too cloudy for solar in the UK

The UK certainly gets its share of grey skies and rainy days, but even on the most overcast days, when there is no direct sunlight, solar panels are still hard at work converting (indirect) daylight to free electricity. In fact, the UK has more solar capacity than sunny Spain!

5. Solar doesn't work at night

This one is not really a myth. Solar panels do not generate any electricity in the dark. But even with solar panels, your home remains connected to the grid, so when your solar panels are not producing any power, you still have electricity. Win win!

6. Solar needs subsidies to survive

Solar is heavily subsidised in the UK, but fossil fuels receive 4 times more subsidies than all renewables put together. But unlike fossil fuels, solar slashes your bills, reduces carbon emissions and democratises energy. Which energy source do you prefer to subsidise?

7. DIY solar is a better option

It's true that DIY solar can save you money upfront. After all, you only have to pay for the price of the system and save a ton on the cost of labour. But remember, installations need to be MCS accredited in order to benefit from the Feed-in Tariff. Also, there are a ton of things to consider when installing solar panels. It takes training and experience to ensure you get the most out of your system in the long run.

8. Solar panels are not green

It does take energy to produce solar panels, so they are not 100% green. But they continue to produce clean energy for decades to come. Even in the UK's overcast skies, the energy payback period for solar panels is less than four years!

9. Alot of maintenance is required by solar panels

Solar panels require very little to no maintenance. This is because they have no moving parts and are designed to be self cleansing from rain water. Something which we have plenty of!

10. Solar panels have a short lifespan

solar panels come with a performance warranty of 25 years! Realistically, a well installed system can far outlive this.

11. Solar panels are ugly

This is a matter of personal taste, but panels nowadays come in a various forms including solar tiles that replace traditional roof tiles. You will hardly be able to tell they are there!

12. It is better to wait until panels are cheaper

It is true that the price of panels has been falling dramatically over the past few decades. But the Feed-in Tariff, the main financial incentive by the UK government is also designed to be degenerative, or to reduce with time. The longer you wait, the less you earn!

13. My property value will decrease with solar

Property value is infact known to increase with solar panels, if the system has been paid for upfront. Usually, the price of the system can be passed on to the new buyer.

14. Solar is a fad and will lose traction

According to the International Energy Agency, solar will be the world's number one electricity source by 2050. More than 500,000 households in the UK have gone solar already. Its a revolution!

15. Going solar is a hassle

Going solar can be a hassle, but if you hire the services of a trusted solar panel installer who is MCS accredited, it can be quite the opposite. Within a day or two, your house can be installed with solar panels generating free electricity for your family for the next 25 years!
