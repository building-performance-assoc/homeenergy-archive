Connecting the Dots at the First Annual HELIX Summit in Boston

By Lindsay Bachman Flickinger · 16.11.2016

On November 10, 2016, Northeast Energy Efficiency Partnerships (NEEP) organized the first annual, one-day HELIX Summit in Boston, MA. The summit brought together industry experts in the energy efficiency, real estate, software and appraisal industries to provide input on the value and direction of the Home Energy Labeling Information Exchange (HELIX) project.

Buying a home is the largest investment most of us will ever make. But home buyers typically receive little or no information about how much energy they will need to power the homes they see listed for sale. Historically, this information was not available. However, scores and labels like Home Energy Score, the HERS index, and the Building Performance Institute’s-2101-S-2013 Standard Requirements for a Certificate of Completion for Residential Energy Efficiency Upgrades are becoming widespread sources of information for home buyers on energy consumption, costs, and the energy efficiency and/or green features of a home. 

To facilitate the automatic transfer of this information to real estate listings, NEEP is developing a database capable of automatically populating Multiple Listings Services (MLS) and other portals like Trulia and Zillow with home energy information from Home Energy Score and other sources when it is available and approved by the seller. As HPC noted in its 2013 report, Unlocking the Value of An Energy-Efficient Home, databases like HELIX provide a more effective means of getting information into the marketplace than relying on homeowners to remember and locate physical certificates. They also have the added benefit of allowing energy efficiency data to be accessed at any time.

HELIX is a the three-year project supported by the U.S. Department of Energy, Vermont Public Service Department, Massachusetts Department of Energy Resources, and several other local, state, and national partners. It will be piloted by several northeastern states, will be HPXML-compliant, and will include outreach and education on the value of home energy information to real estate professionals. The project is expected to be completed in March 2019.
