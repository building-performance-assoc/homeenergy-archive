Share Your ACI Memories

By Stephanie Rex · 15.12.2015

The Home Performance Coalition (HPC) is celebrating 30 years of conferences in 2016, marking a major milestone anniversary for our organization.

We want to share your greatest memories, stories, and takeaways from the last three decades of home performance conferences. Whether it is a story about a connection that grew into a lifelong partnership, a funny memory that could have only taken place at ACI, or something you learned that changed your business—we want to hear about it and we want to share it.

Contact HPC's Marketing Manager, Stephanie Rex, at srex@homeperformance.org to share your story, and it could be included in a series of 30th anniversary blogs, newsletter stories, social media posts, and more. You can also use the following hashtag on social media to share your thoughts: #MyACIMemories

Be sure to join us at the 2016 ACI National Home Performance Conference in Austin to celebrate with us in person!

 

Stephanie Rex is the marketing and communications manager for HPC.
