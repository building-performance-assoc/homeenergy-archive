Home TV Internet Service Providers Fight For your Business

By Tom Clark · 09.11.2012

Internet service providers for home are often the same companies that provide the cable television for the home users. The competition is very hot in this industry. Everyone is pleading for your business. Companies are no longer satisfied with just providing one service for homeowners. To the contrary, these companies want to be the sole provider of all communication and home entertainment networks in your home.

The Bundled Package: Gift or Curse
It appears to be growing at alarming rates. The prices of the bundled packages always start off great. This is how customers are lured from one company to the other. Advertisements come in the mail. Campaigns promote television, phone, and Internet services for less than $100 dollars. It almost seems too good to be true. You may ask yourself why you are paying so much when this new plan is so little. A lot of people switch for the lower rates. It may be a year down the line before they realize that the fine print will bring the rates right back up.

Cable vs. Satellite
The cable companies have had a lot of things locked down in terms of entertainment for years. Cable television was what all people were accustomed to in the city. Rural areas that could not get cable television would settle for satellite. This is how the game was being played decades ago. Today things are very different.

Millions of people in metropolitan areas are picking up on the benefits of satellite television as well. Most of these plans are cheaper than cable. Sometimes you get the same chances, but you don't have to pay the higher cable prices. The problem is that cable companies typically offer better Internet service. This is why the bundle package for both television cable and Internet service are still a great deal. It is difficult for a company with satellite services to overshadow cable companies that offer so much bandwidth. The battle is not stopping though. Satellite and DIRECTV companies have a trick up their sleeves that many cable companies did not foresee.

HD vs. 1080P
Vendors like DIRECTV have thrown blows at the cable companies like Charter and Knology. The representatives for these companies will often tell potential customers something that they may not know: DIRECTV offers Blu-Ray quality 1080p movies. The cable vendors do not. This may seem fabricated, but it is the truth. In the world of high definition television there are some HD channels offered through cable, but it pales in comparison to the 1080p by DIRECTV.

People often see this type of quality when they are in a store. Best Buy, for example, has many televisions that have a brilliant resolution. Many people get the television home and find that they cannot get that same resolution that they saw in the store. The people that are frustrated are the cable users. Cable companies don't provide that intensity of resolution yet. This levels the playing field for satellite companies, but the competitive struggle continues.
