Automated Home Systems and the Next Decade

By Grace Sumrall · 28.03.2013

Changes in technology have automated so many different kinds of processes in virtually every industry today. However, it still has so many more sectors to go. From paying bills from home to sending money online to friends and family, seemingly no industry is left untouched. As energy prices skyrocket, reducing energy usage in the home is one of the areas that manufactures are working on improving, especially within the next decade. The push in this industry is to develop more energy efficient home-automated systems and deploy them as quickly possible.

Government Backed Incentives

The drive to increase these initiatives is backed up by various government agencies with attractive incentives. Which means, more people will benefit from the smarter energy efficient automated systems, since there will be energy cost savings involved and tax reductions due to the installation of innovative solutions.

Automated Home Systems Will Reduce Energy Bills

With automated home systems, homeowners can reduce the amount that they pay on their energy bills each month. One reason for the energy savings is these systems can be programmed automatically to reduce the amount of energy that is needed in the home until peak hours of the day. For instance, if the home is naturally cool during certain times, specifically in the morning hours, the automated cool system can adjust the temperature accordingly. As a result, the amount of energy used in these cooling systems can be reduced substantially during these cooler hours. On the other hand, if no one is in the home during specific hours of the day, these automated systems can turn the cooling systems off.

This same scenario is true for those who want their homes to be warm during the winter months. Which means, the homeowner can set their heating systems to adjust the temperatures during the cold winter nights. These systems can also be programmed to warm the home, specifically during the hours that people return to their homes. Thereby, saving on his or her energy bills when no one is there.

Less than 2% of Homes Equipped with Automated Home Systems

According to information from triplepundit.com, the United States is lacking in numbers in this area because less than 2% of homeowners have a smart grid system or home automation systems installed. To increase these number dramatically within the next few years, many manufactures are expecting to implement energy efficient versions of cooling and heating systems that will be reduced in cost. The costs for these systems are expect to range from $50 to $100, which will be the average. One of the main goals and objectives with the pricing is to make sure it is feasible for homeowners to make an initial investment so that they can get the energy prices in the home down.

Based on information from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, the average household is paying over $100 each month on their electric bills. This is one of the main reasons why the government is offering incentives for various kinds of heating and cooling systems that meet certain required energy specifications. With cutting edge automated energy efficient systems, manufactures are expecting to turn these energy problems around.
