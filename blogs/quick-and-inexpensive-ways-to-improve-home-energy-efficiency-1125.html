Quick And Inexpensive Ways To Improve Home Energy Efficiency

By Megan Arevalo · 23.07.2018

As energy costs keep soaring, the need to have energy efficient home is also on the rise. An infographic by blinds-2go.co.uk states that 39% of household energy costs come from heating and cooling a home. Hence, many homeowners are currently incorporating energy-efficient strategies to reduce costs.

 

However, the desire to go green is not only about cutting costs and ensuring we have a sustainable environment, but also about improving overall comfort. A few simple tweaks can help you attain this desire.

 

Preferably, an overhaul improvement to the entire house would be ideal. But, considering how costly it can be to make major changes to the main energy efficiency determinants, such as lighting, insulation and heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC), you can start with a few quick and cheap measures and then slowly upgrade to the large improvements.  

First, you can make habitual behavioral changes. For example, you can use less hot water by taking quick showers, always turn off the lights in the rooms you’re exiting, and reduce water heater temperature from 140 degrees to a still functional 120 degrees. More so, turn off appliances after use and unplug them too as they can still consume energy when plugged in.

 

Then, you can look at switching to CFL or LED energy saver bulbs, install new thermostat to reduce the amount of fuel needed for heating, and seal the leaks in your walls, foundation slab, ducts, and ceiling.

 

Furthermore, reduce energy costs by buying colored drapes or honeycomb pleated shades to reduce heat gains and provide great insulation. Also, by weatherstripping your doors and windows, you can make your house more energy efficient by 20%, as it reduces the amount of conditioned air leaving through the cracks. Note that doing this can also reduce drafts and cold spots, thus increasing the comfort in your home.
