Purchase Bulk Green and Energy Star Products at Discount Prices!

By Elena Keamy · 26.02.2014

Quantity Quotes is a great tool for builders and contracters looking to save time and money on bulk purchases of energy-efficient products.

Developed by the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), the fast, easy-to-use tool enables everyone, from multifamily building owners and managers to schools and universities to government entities, to negotiate discounted prices directly with suppliers of green and Energy Star-qualified products, including:

  • Light bulbs
  • Light fixtures
  • Clothes washers
  • Dehumidifiers
  • Dishwashers
  • Refrigerators
  • Room air conditioners
  • Insulation (recycled content and low emissions)

To date, nearly 3,300 purchasers and 200 product providers have connected through Quantity Quotes, resulting in nearly half a million energy-efficient products purchased.

Visit Quantity Quotes to search for products and get competitive bids from multiple vendors at once.
