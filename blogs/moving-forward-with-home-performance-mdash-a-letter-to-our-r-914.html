Moving Forward with Home Performance&mdash;A Letter to Our Readers

By Tom White · 17.11.2016
There is still much uncertainty about how the new Trump administration will effect your business and the energy efficiency of U.S homes. But over the last eight years many states, local governments, and utilities have begun major initiatives that will continue to support professionals like you who are working to create high-performance homes.

Home Energy will continue to highlight the business-building proven practices that are moving home performance forward. Because when it comes to creating skilled jobs, contributing to your community, and creating thriving businesses, investing in upgrading existing homes—or building high performance new homes—is always a winner.
 
By making homes more energy efficient, you are helping all U.S. citizens stay healthier, more comfortable, and keep more of their hard-earned wages. Since 1984, Home Energy has provided our readers with reliable data and building science that show which home performance techniques and technologies really work in actual homes, and why.

We look forward to serving you, and working with the new federal administration, to create healthier, more energy efficient homes.

Thank you for your readership and support,

Tom White
Publisher
