Energy Exchange and Energy ConneXions Trade Show

By Andrew Hunt · 29.06.2015

The Energy Exchange – below - will be providing two-and-a-half days of structured training to employees from federal agencies, both civilian and military, as well as partnering organizations, who are working to improve facility performance, advance the use of renewable energy, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions. 

 

This looks like a great learning opportunity for those involved in these areas. More information is below:

ENERGY CONNEXIONS

TRADE SHOW 

PHOENIX CONVENTION CENTER  

Phoenix, Arizona 

  

AUGUST 10-12, 2015

Technology and service suppliers of all sizes will be on hand to answer questions and provide demonstrations at Energy ConneXions. Discover a variety of tools and equipment for improving outcomes and reducing costs. Energy ConneXions will feature solutions for lighting, the building envelope, and more!

  • Preview next generation technologies at the emerging technology pavilion.
  • Be sure to visit the technology theater, featuring new products and innovations from the trade show.
  • Make the most of your trade show visit and join in hands-on, interactive learning experiences.

 

TRADE SHOW HOURS

 

August 10: 5 pm - 7 pm Opening Reception

August 11: 10 am - 4 pm

August 12: 10 am - 2 pm

 

Exhibitors: Space is limited. Visit the  EnergyConneXions website to learn more or contact MC2: 678-398-2650,  info@2015energyconnexions.com  

Energy Exchange attendees: This is a free event.
