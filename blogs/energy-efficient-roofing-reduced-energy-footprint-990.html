Energy Efficient Roofing reduced energy footprint

By Ralph Richardson · 12.04.2017

There is a new energy efficient, insulated roof covering launching in 2017 that helps keep the attic cool in summer and warmer in winter. The lightweight can retrofit any home and for new construction, delivers an elegant solution to the Title 24 high performance attic. According the the Florida Solar Energy Center - FSEC, in hot climates re-roofing with EternaTile can reduce the energy footprint of an existing home 20-35%. Energy Efficiency in here to stay and dramatic improvements are on the horizon to energize this growing trend.FSEC Full Report
