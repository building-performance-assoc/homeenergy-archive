Solar collector efficiency over time

By Henry · 01.12.2015

Contractor installs 5.8 KW solar system, at different angles and facing various direction- what is the real generated output? Collector efficiencies drop off with age but how much? Data provided to the homeowner is lab generated. How does the customer determined lost efficiency? ABB offers a energy generated software for there inverters but it only looks at 12 months and doesn't compare year to year. Is there better measuring software that is inexpensive?
