HVAC Is Facing An Uphill Battle For Its Adoption

By Amanda peet · 11.03.2016

At present, HVAC is facing a serious battle for adoption. However, there are some lower cost options available, matching flexible clients’ requirements.

Buildings are noted to consume 38% of primary energy in developing the world, where HVAC accounts for nearly 13%. Therefore, concerns are increasing over resource scarcity, costs and climate change, which are further driving interest in improving the present HVAC efficiency. Recent reports examined the complex landscape, related to HVAC technologies. This report talks about the emerging technologies and rank establishments to identify some of the latest opportunities with true growth potential.

Notes from the Technology department:

Even though the technology department is quite evolutionary in HVAC, still disruptive technologies persist. The inclusion of such disruptive technologies can reduce the present building energy consumption, at a significant rate. Recent HVAC equipment adoption is making some revolutionary changes, and some promising technologies comprise of ability to reduce the HVAC usage costing.

Faces a battle for adoption:

At present, the energy efficient HVAC system is facing a battle for the adoption. However, some of the lower cost options are currently making this technology attractive to some leading companies. As mentioned earlier, these systems are taking 13% of the primary energy generated globally. The systems are recently trying out energy efficient equipment, which comprises of absorption chillers, heat pumps, evaporative chillers and even condensing boilers.

Technicalities are somewhat slow:

Recently, the take-up of such technicalities proves to be slow, despite the looming carbon regulations and rising energy cost all over the over. However, this field has seen demands for components, which help in improving the current operation cost along with the performance of the present HVAC equipment. These offers are now producing fastest ROI. Some of these technologies are electronic expansion valves, variable speed compressors, heat recycling and advanced humidity removal. Moreover, this package also comprises of thermal energy storage and variable frequency drives.

Cutting edge HVAC technologies:

Even though some owners and builders will plan to install the complete cutting-edge HVAC system, building managers and other occupants are looking forward to favoring some of the lower cost solutions, based on incremental components. It helps in improving the currently existing systems and their efficiency levels. Component technologies help in demonstrating the fastest return on investment policies, which soothe their path to adoption.

Understanding some of the key findings:

The current report evaluates HVAC components in a separate manner and scores technologies on their maturity level and technical value. It then helps to plot the relative potential of each technology on matrix. This matrix comprises of four quadrants: future winners, current winners, faded incumbents and long shots. Some of the key findings are:

  • Heat pumps are likely to show some of the particular efficiency potentials when finally coupled with present water heaters. Some of the leading firms primarily offer this kind of approach, globally.
  • Advanced forms of HVAC systems are now checking out on incremental adoption. Both national and internal companies are further adopting some of the efficient gas fired furnaces.
  • There are additions of some new technologies as well, like advanced heat pumps and radiant heating. The main aim is to replace some of the old steam heating boilers and other standard furnaces with the new technicalities.
  • The current value of HVAC is likely to exceed the sum of spare parts. Some of the highest ranking HVAC technicalities, report to deliver better returns when combined.

Get along with radiant heaters:

Loads of opportunities are waiting for advanced HVAC parts to present their values. At present, low costing variable frequency, the drive is defined as the current winner in recent reports. On the other hand, the expansion valves are termed as future winners. Radiant construction heaters are best for adding heat to home during addition or construction project. These are cost effective in nature and come with eco-friendly guides.

 

Amanda is offering clear vision on the latest trends surrounding HVAC.
