Superfan wins the title “The Most Innovative Energy Saving Product”

By superfan · 03.10.2015

Superfan wins CII National Energy Award 2015 as Most Innovative Energy Saving Product.

The objective of the awards is to recognize and Award “Excellence” in Energy Management in Industries and to facilitate sharing of information by excellent energy efficient companies.

About CII

The Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) is a non-government, not-for-profit, industry-led and industry-managed organization, playing a proactive role in India´s development process.

CII works to create and sustain an environment conducive to the development of India, partnering industry, Government, and civil society, through advisory and consultative processes. Founded in 1895, India's premier business association has over 7900 members, from the private as well as public sectors and an indirect membership of over 200,000 enterprises from around 240 national and regional sectoral industry bodies.

Unique features of Superfan

Superfan has brought innovation to a highly common domestic product that is used by millions of households by saving valuable energy, adding more comforts and eliminating many pain-points for consumers.

The unique features of superfan are,

  • Eco-friendly – Superfans are packed in eco-friendly material, avoiding totally plastic and thermocol.
  • Consume far less power than other fans.
  • Saves your Money.
  • Operated through remote control.
  • Available in different colours
  • Good performance compared to other ordinary fans. 

Superfan’s Performance

Ordinary fans available in the market do not match the performances of superfan. Since they consume less energy, they run twice longer than the other fans on inverters and UPS. Superfans consume just 4, 14, and 35 watts at low, medium and high speed respectively. On the other hand, ‘ordinary’ fans in the market consume 12, 39, and 75 watts at low, medium and high speed respectively. Superfans run even on low voltage in full speed, when other fans don’t even start.

If the existing ceiling fans in India are replaced by Superfan, the demand can come down from 13650MW to 4900MW. A huge reduction of 8750MW for the nation.

The Managing Director of the Versa Drives, Mr. Sundar Muruganandhan says “Our experience of 24 years in industrial products has helped in making a robust design for reliable and long lasting ceiling fan. With this we want to create awareness in customer’s mind to think more about ceiling fans than normally they do.”
