Software Enables Utilities in the Water Sector to Automatically Identify Leaks and Analyze Consumption

By Pedro Mendes · 29.01.2014

Water losses and unbilled consumption represent a significant percentage of the water consumed in our networks and, consequently, a worrying cost for Utilities in the water sector. The Kisense Water software presents itself as a response to this challenge, by adding features like intelligent analysis of night flows to the benefits of traditional telemetry systems.

 Using the current telemetry solutions, Utilities in the water sector can get detailed and remote information about the consumption of each area that is measured and controlled by the solution, as well as the consumption of the clients associated to these areas. The Kisense Water adds an analytical component to these features, which enables automatic crossing and analysis of the collected data.

This analysis allows, among other advantages, to detect water losses (real and apparent) by comparing the consumption measured in clients with the water flows that enter the measured and controlled areas of the telemetry system. Even in places without consumption measurement, the advanced Kisense Water algorithms - through the Systems Management module - provide leak detection, based on the flow analysis of the measured and controlled areas, as well as the quantification of the leak’s dimension.

The information is made available to users through a web platform that is accessible from anywhere. This platform is also prepared to receive data from other systems that the Utility has already installed.

Through the Smart Metering module - dedicated to telemetry data analysis - the Kisense Water brings Utilities in the water sector benefits such as: cost reduction due to being able to control unauthorized uses, cost reduction associated with manual meter readings, as well as better water meter management and an improvement of customer relations.

The implementation of telemetry data analysis tools, which can be used to support decision-making, is a daily need for Utilities. These tools enable a more efficient and effective answer to the challenges experienced by these entities, both as an operational component, as well as in management terms.

Kisense Water, developed by ISA - Intelligent Sensing Anywhere, is a tool for managing the entire urban water cycle - water supply, rainwater drainage and wastewater disposal – with the development of the platform beginning by focusing on modules dedicated to the water supply management.

 

Pedro Mendes is the product and marketing director for Kisense Water.
