3 Common Plumbing Mistakes Homeowners Make

Plumbing problems are common issues that homeowner face throughout their life, often catching us off guard. It’s important to keep yourself educated, and even try to learn how to fix some of these problems yourself. This can help you save time and money, and give you the skill and knowledge for future use. Sadly, things don’t always go as planned. Even worse, when it comes to  plumbing repairs going wrong, things can go from bad to worse, real fast! Avoid the following mistakes and prevent your repair job from turning into a costly, catastrophic mess!

 

 Don’t Forget to Shut Off the Water  

` Before you start any plumbing project, be sure to shut off the water supply to your home. The shut off valve is usually located next to your water meter, and will stop water flow to any of your appliances and fixtures. This can prevent costly flooding and water damages in the case of an emergency.  

 

Make Sure You Are Prepared

When attempting a DIY plumbing project, you want to make sure you have the right tools and and are taking the necessary safety precautions. When choosing your pipes and connectors, you don’t want to mix up pieces or choose the wrong size. Make sure your materials are all up to code and quality made. And make sure you always put your safety first! These few steps can help you fix that plumbing problem  in no time!  

 

Know When to Ask for Help

Often an issue of pride or perseverance, you should know when it's time to throw in the towel and call for backup. Although a repair may seem simple, if something goes wrong, you don’t want to make the problem worse. This can lead to further damages and expensive repairs. There’s nothing wrong with calling a plumber for help, or acknowledging that a project is past your abilities.
