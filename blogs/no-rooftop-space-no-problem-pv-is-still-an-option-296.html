No Rooftop Space, No problem. PV is Still an Option!

By Christy Maraone · 16.08.2012

Building-owners are always looking for ways to optimize unused space and save money in energy costs and Solar PV is a great way to achieve both.  Renewable energy financing strategies, such as utilizing Solar PV, is one way to attain your energy saving goals. Solar PV offers an ROI of 5-7 years, increases property values and EnergyStar scores, and boasts tremendous marketing and environmental benefits.  As a home- and building-owner, solar PV makes sense, but what if you don’t have the ideal set up for traditional rooftop solar? 

 Crystalline offers a variety of benefits and works in many different applications.  Crystalline is the most popular, long-standing PV product on the market with a proven track record.  It works in both ballasted and penetrated options and is hail resistant.  It is also angled so snow can quickly melt off and the panels can start producing energy.

 Crystalline works in a variety of applications:

 -          Rooftop:

  • This is the most traditional application as it’s a terrific way to use unused space and make it an energy producing asset.  Penetrated and ballasted options may be available.

-          Ground mount:

  • Vacant land can be transformed into an energy producing asset. 

-          Carport structures:

  • Carport structures not only provides shade for cars, it can also be an energy producing asset when solar is added. 

-          Awnings:

  • Why stop at providing shade for your building when you can also make your awnings energy efficient with solar PV. 

***If you have an older roof, ground mount, carport structures, and awnings are the better options.  Rooftop is feasible only if the roof is replaced before the solar installation takes place.

 Crystalline is a versatile product that can be used in a variety of different applications.  If your rooftop is shaded or facing the wrong direction, don’t fret.  One of these other options may work instead.  Talk with a solar contractor today to see what options are available concerning solar for business  at your building or land space.
