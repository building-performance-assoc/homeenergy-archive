'To be energy wise, cut your use to size'

By sameer khare · 11.08.2012

 

 

 

Whatever be the kind of energy, it is involved in almost all the outgoing processes. Almost all the existing sectors have a huge involvement of energy.

 As the years are passing by, the demand for energy is shooting up, thus putting more demand pressure on all energy resources basically on the renewables. So to ease this pressure, use energy wisely so that much time is available to develop efficient technologies to tap the renewable sources in a better way. The world population & energy consumption per capita is increasing at the rate of 2% per year, so net world’s energy consumption is increasing at the rate of 4% per year i.e it doubles after every 36 years. So today, we are at a time when the most rational classes among us have realised that worst of our fears regarding energy may actually come true. There is a mad hunt for energy, to meet the growing global demand, that is primarily responsible for this. And as we have limited energy sources, extraction & usage of energy should be in a planned way. So, every individual must have a cautious, vigilant approach towards the consumption of energy. Basically, obtaining the maximum output from the available resources is being energy wise. Set a particular quantum of energy and use that much only i.e decide your usage depending on the necessity, because if we consume excess quantity to our need , we will ultimately be running short of these energy sources and then “don’t say that we didn’t warn you, so save these resources as it is not going to last forever”. Therefore, it is very important for every individual to make use of these resources very prudently & with a scientific approach to get maximum benefit out of them.
