V.P. Biden Announces Three New Recovery through Retrofit Initiatives

By Tom White · 09.11.2010
Vice President Biden announced new Recovery through Retrofit initiatives that will grow the home performance industry and help middle-class families save money on their energy bills:

Home Energy Score:
http://www.homeenergyscore.gov

FHA PowerSaver Financing Loans for Homeowners:
http://www.hud.gov/offices/hsg/sfh/title/ti_home.cfm

Workforce Guidelines for Home Energy Upgrades:
http://www.weatherization.energy.gov/retrofit_guidelines

Healthy Indoor Environment Protocols for Home Energy Upgrades:
http://www.epa.gov/iaq/homes/retrofits.html

Small Business Administration’s Green Business Opportunities Small Business Guide:
http://www.sba.gov/training
