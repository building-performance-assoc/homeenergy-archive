PECO & CLEAResult to Host the 2018 HPC National Home Performance Conference & Trade Show in Philadelphia

By Lindsay Bochman · 21.03.2018

March 21, 2018 – Philadelphia, PA – Today we are proud to announce that PECO and CLEAResult will host the 2018 HPC National Home Performance Conference & Trade Show at the Philadelphia Downtown Marriott, April 23-26, 2018. PECO and CLEAResult play important roles in the energy efficiency community and have demonstrated their commitment to home performance and weatherization through many years of involvement.

PECO is Pennsylvania’s largest electric and natural gas utility with a long history supporting energy efficiency. At the 1994 home performance conference in Philadelphia, PECO was a sustaining sponsor. “It’s thrilling to see how their commitment to home performance and weatherization has continued to grow, as well as their commitment to our organization, 25 years later,” said Nate Natale, HPC’s Vice President of Education & Events.

Likewise, CLEAResult is the single largest provider of energy solutions in North America, playing an important role in the residential energy efficiency industry. “We are fortunate to have such a good relationship with this company and many of the talented people it employs. This will be the second time in the past three years that CLEAResult has been a host of our national conference and I think that speaks volumes,” said Natale.

It is a powerful statement to have both PECO and CLEAResult team up to host this event. HPC offers the industry's leading educational events and trade shows in partnership with the nation's leading experts in home performance and weatherization. To learn more about this conference, visithttp://www.homeperformance.org/conferences/HPC18.

 ***

ABOUT PECO: Headquartered in Philadelphia, PECO is Pennsylvania’s largest electric and natural gas utility, delivering power to more than 1.6 million electric customers and more than 511,000 natural gas customers in southeastern Pennsylvania. PECO is a subsidiary of Exelon Corporation (NYSE: EXC), the nation’s leading competitive energy supplier​.​

ABOUT CLEAResult: CLEAResult is the single largest provider of energy solutions in North America. With offices in over 60 cities in the U.S. and Canada, they tailor efforts to fit clients' specific needs. Their energy experts design and maintain energy optimization services for utility companies as well as institutional, commercial and industrial organizations. They deliver solutions that lower load requirements for utilities, reduce energy bills for end users and minimize environmental burdens on communities. CLEAResult changes the way people use energy.

ABOUT HPC: HPC is a 501c3 nonprofit organization that advances the residential energy efficiency market through research, education, and policy analysis. HPC works with industry leaders in the home performance and weatherization industries and collaborates with like-minded organizations to help ensure all homes are healthy, comfortable, and energy-efficient. For more information, please visit www.homeperformance.org.
