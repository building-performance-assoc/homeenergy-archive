The Green Smart House: Top Sustainable Design Choices

By Matt Lee · 12.07.2018

As technology and awareness advances, so do the sustainable design options for your home. Whether you’re wanting to start from scratch or you want to update an existing home, there are many options to choose from. Homeowners no longer just want to use sustainable materials, they also want to make sure their home is a “smart” home. This means you can use technology in your home to make sure it is comfortable and green all at the same time.

There are several sustainable design options out there that you can choose from. Take a look at the options below and see how you can help change your home to be green and smart at the same time.

Fiber Cement Siding

When you want to change the look of your home, you want to make sure that you are using materials that are certified sustainable. Fiber cement siding is just that product. Not only is it a great material to use for a sustainable design, it is also very low-maintenance. It is a durable product that will last for a long time making it easy to keep your home looking its best.

Green Smart House Options

A smart home is a newer trend that everyone is interested in. This basically means your home is set up for you to control different aspects such as your HVAC system and lightning. You can control the temperature when you’re not there or you can even see who is dropping packages off at your home with a variety of camera setups. This smart home design is a great way to make sure you’re reducing your carbon footprint by using the HVAC system when you need to and not cooling or heating when everyone has left the home for the day. It also allows you to turn on lights to protect your home when you’re on vacation or away for business.

Real Wood Siding

Most everyone loves the look of real wood whether it is in the home as flooring or on the outside of the home such as a log cabin style. While real wood is beautiful and warm, it also brings about issues that are difficult to take care of. It can bring along wood pests that can destroy your home and it is also removing much-needed trees from the area that you may want to avoid. Real wood siding is a way you can get that warm, beautiful wood look without all the hassle that comes with it. This siding offers a durable way to protect your home with added insulation and a beautiful wood exterior. It is also highly resistant to the wear and tear of the environment around it. Where this would normally cause you to replace real wood, this siding doesn’t have to be replaced.

Steel Siding Homes

Another way to get a sleek, beautiful design and use sustainable materials is with steel siding. Steel siding homes offer a unique look and protection to your home. They also offer a way for your home to be even more green by collecting rainwater. You can set it up to use rainwater in your home and steel siding can help you to collect it to use in various aspects. This not only gives you a beautiful appealing exterior, it also helps you make wise sustainable design choices for generations to come.  

Sustainable Acoustic Solutions

If you have an exciting home theater setup in your family room, you may want to consider some sustainable acoustic solutions to help you keep the proper sound levels. There are acoustic panels that can be installed to help you keep the sound level at a comfortable volume and get the most out of your theater system. You can also discover sustainable ceiling treatments as well to help keep the sound clear and crisp while being eco-friendly at the same time.

These are just a few of the smart house design options that will help you go green and keep your home comfortable and livable. Check out some of the smart house options available to you if you do not already have a smart home to see what fits your lifestyle best.
