5 benefits of using an evaporative cooler

By Wynand Coetze · 07.06.2017

We all want our homes and living spaces to have cool and clean air, the lifestyle advantages that come from traditional air-conditioning are numerous and enjoyable. While everyone bakes in the summer heat, you stay indoors and relax in a temperate climate that suits your preference.

One of the drawbacks of owning an air-conditioned home is the incredible expense of cooling. The energy required by a central air-conditioning system can sometime consume 60% of your monthly power utility bill. This adds up over the course of a hot summer season into thousands that you can save by using an alternative technology that is just as effective.

Evaporative cooling is making waves in the air cooling industry. A technology that operates on a principle that is as old as time itself, evaporative cooling offers effective cooling at a fraction of the price, while remaining environmentally friendly and reducing your homes carbon footprint.

Sound interesting? Would you be keen to save money and stay cool with evaporative cooling? Here’s some other great information and benefits for using evaporative cooling in your home.

How it works

As we mentioned already, evaporative cooling works on an age-old principle, that was gifted to us by nature; evaporation. A lot goes on when water evaporates, have you ever sat beside a dam on a hot Saturday and then felt a cool breeze wash over your skin? That’s a natural example of evaporative cooling at work.

Today, evaporative coolers are manufactured to achieve the same ambition as the breeze, cool things down when its hot outside. They achieve this by pulling in air from outdoors with a fan and drawing it through a set of cooling pads that are constructed from an absorptive material. This moist material forces the air to undergo a phase change while it cools it down and the resultant latent heat exchange between the air and the water results in the air dropping temperature as the excess energy is absorbed by the water.

The cool air is then pushed out through the ducting system into the room or rooms of the house. There are no refrigeration circuits as in traditional air-conditioners and the only components that require electricity to get the unit to work is a water pump, fan and controller unit, making for a much lower energy demand as opposed to a traditional air-conditioning system.   1. Choice and installation

With Evap coolers being the new trend in air-cooling technology, manufacturers are producing incredible coolers and there are model variety’s that can suit any application. From massive HVAC systems that are used in combination with 2-phase systems for climate control of corporate buildings and skyscrapers, to small portable units that can be wheelied into any room of the home, evaporative coolers are available in smart, sleek designs for absolutely any form of cooling that can be found.

Installation of a unit for the home is relatively simple, with a choice of a central system, fitted by a professional, or a window mounted unit that pops right into the window frame, right down to a portable unit that can easily cool a room the size of an average bedroom and then be wheeled around the home on a set of casters.

Whatever your application, take a search around the internet and have a look for a dealer that has a great supply network and service guarantee alongside a 2 year manufacturer’s warranty.

  1. Eco-friendly operation

Being environmentally conscious and responsible is no longer something that only tree-hugging hippies have to be concerned with. Everyone is completely responsible and accountable for their carbon impact on the planet. This is especially true for people using cooling systems in their homes. Using a energy-sucking appliance to cool your home is simply irresponsible to the environment, however, using and evaporative cooler is a much less demanding on mother nature, in essence you are using mother nature’s principles to reduce your reliance on modern carbon energy, sounds beautiful doesn’t it?

  1. Low operating cost

Alongside the energy savings comes a welcomed relief, energy costs money and your budget currently includes cooling, so you are going to save right off the bat on your monthly bill. It’s not uncommon for households that convert to evap cooling from traditional aircon to save up to 60% of there seasonal electricity bill. This is where things get real, reductions in your monthly budget on your energy bill can be better allocated to other areas of your household budget or savings, really impacting your bottom line.

  1. Simple maintenance

Evaporative coolers are not a hassle in the maintenance department either, they have few working parts other that the pump, fan and control panel. This means that chances of mechanical failure are reduced during the cooling reason in the summer. Having a breakdown on your AC can be devastating on a hot day, especially if you are acclimatised to air-con living, things can get pretty uncomfortable, pretty quickly to say the least.

You can clean and do basic maintenance on your evaporative cooler yourself, checking that the filter is clean, the pads are in good condition and absorbing water and that the pump works. Most of these parts cost very little to replace if they are worn, the only big cost would be replacing the LCD control panel but chances of this part of the unit failing are slim.

Every so often before the start of the summer season, you should take your unit to a service centre for a check-up. If there is any mechanical error with your unit, they will pick it up and do some preventative maintenance that will keep your unit cooling all summer long.

  1. Fresh, cool, low-cost air

This sums it up. What if offers you is everything you can expect in an air-con system, with the low-cost savings and clean air advantages of using free-flowing air systems in your home. Evaporative coolers do not operate in the same way that air-cons do in the sense that they require your windows and doors to be left open to create negative pressure in the room and allow the hot, exhaust air to escape.  Make sure you apply correct maintenance and repairs to your system as you will ensure a longer last life for all of your systems.

This becomes an issue for people that live in humid environments such as next to lakes and the Oceanside. The already humid atmosphere causes the unit and the airflow to become saturated, dropping the effectiveness and efficiency of the cooler. This can be combated by using a 2-stage evaporative cooler that removes the moisture from the air after it cools it down, sort of acting like a dehumidifier within the system and keeping the air-drier, alas it usually comes with a higher price tag and is a little bit more energy intensive to operate.

 

The conclusion

Using an evaporative cooler as an alternative cooling solution in your home just makes good sense. From the cost savings to the environmental benefits, evaporative cooling is fast becoming the new global standard in air-cooling allowing us to take back control of our living environments while remaining energy conscious and creating savings in our monthly household budgets.
