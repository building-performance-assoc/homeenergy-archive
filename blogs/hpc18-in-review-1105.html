HPC18 - In Review

By Lindsay Bachman, HPC Director of Marketing & Communications · 10.05.2018

At this year's national conference and trade show we networked, learned, laughed and grew! Thank you for sharing your week with us.

To recap, together we:

  • tested and learned about products
  • attended tracks and session on a myriad of topics
  • toured Philly with ECA & MaGrann
  • 'happy houred' with Women In Building Performance & NEST
  • networked at the trade show
  • heard from Cynthia Adams, this years Leadership Award winner
  • were inspired by Mike Rogers to include women and diversity in our workplaces
  • made purchasing decisions with exhibitors
  • joined Efficiency First
  • drank copious amounts of coffee
  • participated in workshops
  • raffled for a TEC DG-1000
  • cheered on the Phillies
  • boogied with the conference band
  • got a free head shot with Faces of EE
  • made tons of friends
  • traveled to DC for contractor lobby day
  • received a total of 22 possible CEUs from BPI, NARI, ASHI, InterNACHI, Nate
  • explored the awesome city of Philadelphia
  • became a more confident, successful leader in the home performance industry
  • & so much more!
