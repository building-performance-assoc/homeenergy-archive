7 Best Smart Air Conditioners for 2019 & Beyond

By Muhammad Abdullah Hashmi · 16.12.2019

7 Best Smart Air Conditioners for 2019 & Beyond

 

Imagine a home where you don’t have to worry about accidentally leaving your AC running when leaving the house in a hurry, or coming back to a home where you find perfect room temperature, automatically set in your absence. The basic smart air conditioning is controlling your air conditioner from anywhere; the real smart air conditioning is a combo of smart features which automatically adds convenience to your heating/cooling needs and gives boost to your home energy savings.

Wi-Fi connected smart air conditioners are equipped with cutting-edge technology, allowing you to easily maintain the perfect room climate. Convenience and flexibility is the name of the game these days, and smart air conditioners bring you exactly that; providing you complete control over your air conditioner from anywhere via smartphone.

Multiple smart AC options are available and navigating the smart air conditioner market can be a bit daunting. I’ll try to help you out and go over some of the best offerings in the market, covering window ACs, portables, and high wall mount split air conditioners, and let you know what to keep in mind when buying your new smart air conditioner.

Here is a comprehensive guide, detailing the features that you need to keep in mind for your next air conditioner purchase.

What to Look Out for while Buying Your Smart Air Conditioner?

There are certain features which are a must have in a smart air conditioner. Before making the big purchase, you need to be well informed to be able to make the best possible decision.

Depending on the AC manufacturer and the model, there are multiple features on offer. All smart air conditioners provide Wi-Fi functionality, allowing you to control them through a mobile or web app. The standard smart features include location controls, scheduling, AC controls like fan speed, swing position and temperature control. Apart from these standard features, you must give special emphasis to following extra features so that the ROI on your new smart air conditioner is great. Here is the list with brief detail:

 

1.     Smart Home Integration

If you are looking for a smart air conditioner, you are definitely inside the IoT sphere. You might have other smart home devices such as Amazon Alexa or Google Home. You would want your AC to be controlled using voice control through any one of those devices, and very rightly so. The smart air conditioning system, which do not have compatibility with these major voice assistant platforms are at a great disadvantage.

2.     Ease of Use

Most manufacturers now provide mobile and web apps to remotely control your smart AC, as long as it is connected to your Wi-Fi. This gives you global control over your appliance. A major concern when buying such an appliance is the ease of use of the apps, their design, features, responsiveness etc. Another point to ponder over is the installation process. Some manufacturers use third party apps for use with their appliances, which can occasionally result in software and usage issues. Be careful to check this beforehand, and get a feel for the app before making the final decision.

3.     AC Energy Consumption and Ratings

This feature or spec is not applicable to smart AC only but to any type of AC, you are about to buy. We’ve all heard of BTUs, but what are they? British Thermal Units are a measure of an air conditioner’s cooling or heating capacity. The higher the BTUs the more cooling or heating power a unit has. Common BTU capacities range from 6,000 to 12,000 which are suitable for a common sized living room. You must look for an AC that is suitable to the size of your room to consume the least amount of energy while proving the best amount of cooling or heating.

Another factor to look out for is whether an air conditioner is Energy Star certified or not? An Energy Star compliant appliance indicates implementation of more environmentally friendly, and energy saving features within the device. This results in a marked reduction in cooling and heating bills. According to the US EPA, Energy Star qualified room air conditioners are at least 10% more energy efficient than the minimum U.S. federal government standards.

So, while buying your smart AC, do consider the BTU rating and energy certifications.

4.     Cost of the Smart Air Conditioner

Cost is one of the most decisive factor when deciding whether to buy a new appliance or not. Prices vary greatly, depending on cooling capacity, and functionalities. Prices for such products range from $300 to $3000 and can go even higher. The initial investment on smart ACs is of course more than their “dumb” counterparts, but this initial cost can be offset through energy savings across the lifetime of the AC.

Give your Old Air Conditioner the Smartness it Deserves

In case you are not yet ready to invest in a completely new air conditioner, you can easily make your old AC smart! Aftermarket “smart AC controllers” are third party add-ons, which make your dumb air conditioner smart. These nifty little devices are quick to setup, ergonomically designed, and pack a host of extra features as compared to their in-built counterparts.

While LG, JHS, Kuhl and others provide exceptional smart air conditioners, they are still new entrants in the market. These manufacturers have hundreds of popular models that do not deploy the same Wi-Fi compatible features, and are already in use by millions across the US. A few of the models without Wi-Fi compatibility are:

  1. LG LW8019ER 8,200 BTU Window Air Conditioner
  2. Frigidaire FFRE063WAE 6,000 BTU Window Mounted Air Conditioner
  3. Friedrich Chill® 6,000 – 23,500 BTU Window Mounted Air Conditioner
  4. JHS A018-14KR/C 14,000 BTU Portable Air Conditioner
  5. Daikin NV Series Wall mount
  6. Samsung Boracay Wall mounted split air conditioners

 

All of these models, and countless others, with the use of smart controllers, can be provided with all the power of smart ACs and even more. In fact, aftermarket smart controllers can also be used with smart ACs to provide additional features such as live air filter status, humidity and temperature based triggers, and much more. One of the market leaders in aftermarket smart AC controllers is the Cielo Breez Plus.

How to make your old room air conditioner smart?

Cielo Breez Plus – The Perfect Smart AC Controller

Cielo Breez Plus is a Smart Wi-Fi AC controller, used to serve as a Wi-Fi adapter for existing “dumb” air conditioners. Cielo Breez Plus Smart Air Conditioner controller works with mini splits, portable ACs, and Window ACs that have a remote control.

It connects to the Wi-Fi router and lets you control your ACs with your smartphone, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, or indeed the Breez Plus device itself. It brings along with it an impressive database of over 2000 different air conditioner models with which it is compatible, and there’s more being added every day.

Key features:

Let’s go about some of the main features of the Cielo Breez Plus:

  • Comfy Mode: You could set up the Breez Plus to maintain a fixed temperature or humidity range, and it senses those pre-set triggers in the environment. If they deviate, the device automatically applies corrective actions to maintain it at the desired level. This handy function eliminates human intervention, which can in some occasions be impossible, such as when you have pets at home and you are outside, or when you are sleeping.

 

  • Geo Fencing: The device can sense your location through your smartphone, and can either turn your AC on or off, depending upon your proximity from home, so you already have the perfect environment in your living room to relax in when you get back.

 

  • Smart Home Integration: Breez Plus is compatible with Amazon Alexa, and Google Assistant.

 

  • Automatic remote detection: You don’t have to worry about pairing it up. Breez Plus does it for you. In addition, the Cielo Breez Plus device also keep track of all the changes you perform through your OEM remote, to keep the device in sync with your AC at all times.

 

The Cielo Breez Plus comes in at $119.00, and this is definitely an investment which you’d want to make if you are wanting to make your existing air conditioner smart.

 

To sum it up, the world of home appliances is now going to the internet cloud. Remote access is often the first thing which a user demands, and Wi-Fi connectivity is just one step in that domain. More and more manufacturers are now incorporating built in connectivity features, mobile based apps, and functions within them to enable a complete experience for the user.

There are plenty of options to be had in the ductless smart air conditioning category, with portable, high wall mounted, or window air conditioners available from a plethora of manufacturers. But what about the millions of already installed dumb air conditioners or the new air conditioners, which are not smart? The Cielo Breez Plus provides an aftermarket solution for existing customers who would want to get an upgrade, but without the large investment of a new ductless system.

Welcome to the world of smart air conditioning!
