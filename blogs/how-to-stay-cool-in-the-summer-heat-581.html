How to Stay Cool in the Summer Heat

By Chloe Flower · 25.06.2014

Many people dread the blistering summer heat and choose to stay indoors to take advantage of the air conditioning pouring from their vents. Many others decide that being cooped up indoors all summer long is pure torture. So how do they do survive it?

 

• Find the nearest body of water & cool off: Swim, tube, boat, kayak or any other water sport to your heart is content. If you don’t have the luxury of being near a watering hole, a cold shower will work just as well, but it might not be as fun.

 

• Do outside chores or exercise in the morning or early evening: Cut grass, garden, exercise e.g. hike or run, etc. the earlier in the morning the better before the sun has a chance to peek through the clouds.

 

• Don’t use certain appliances during the summer: Abstain from using the dryer (hang dry your clothes instead) and oven (buy fresh baked products at a bakery instead) as these make your air conditioner work double time to keep your home cool which puts a strain on your electric bill and could add up to costly air conditioner repair bills.

 

• Find the nearest shade: When outdoors, find the nearest shady spot whether it be a tree or a man-made product e.g. umbrella, hat etc. When indoors, keep your blinds closed to prevent the sun from heating up your home especially on the windows facing the south and the west.

 

• Eat or drink something cold: When most children think of summer, they imagine swimming in a body of water e.g. beach, lake or river with an ice cream or popsicle in their hand; adults are somewhat similar but usually it involves sipping on a cold, adult beverage e.g. margarita or beer while swimming. But make sure that you drink plenty of water because it is very easy to get dehydrated before you realize it in the scorching summer sun.

 

• Get your air conditioning unit serviced: Before your air conditioning decides to go on the fritz in the intense summer heat, call a service repairman to make sure that your air conditioner is working properly and running efficiently.

 

• Make home repairs promptly: Make sure that your attic is sealed and has plenty of insulation to keep your home cool because it can reach 150 degrees in the hot summer months. Vent the attic, which will drop the temperature up there by at least 10 degrees. Also make sure all of your doors, windows and ducts are sealed too to prevent the cold air from leaking out and keeping the hot air along with the humidity outside.  Also get your roofer to install light colored shingles on your roof instead of the darker ones, which absorb heat and in turn will make your home hotter.

 

• Use fans whenever possible: Whether it is ceiling fans to help circulate the air more in your home and take some of the pressure off of your air conditioner or whether it is handheld fans to bring you some relief while outdoors, fans are a great way to beat the heat before it gets unbearable.
