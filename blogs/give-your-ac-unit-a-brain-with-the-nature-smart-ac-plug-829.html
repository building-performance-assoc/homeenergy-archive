Give Your AC Unit a Brain With the Nature Smart AC Plug

By Josh Lehman · 20.04.2016

Room air conditioners are responsible for our comfort, and for the largest portion of our electricity bills. That is why a team of Harvard /MIT students have started Nature Global, a home energy management company that is launching an intelligent controller for room air conditioners worldwide.

The Nature Smart AC Plug, which will be launched in the US this summer, is designed to integrate with any window, through-wall, or mini-split air conditioner. You simply plug it into your power outlet, and then plug your AC unit into the Smart AC Plug.

Popular smart thermostats, such as the Nest Learning Thermostat, do not yet have a way to control room-based air conditioning systems.

The Smart AC Plug has an app that allows users to control their AC unit from anywhere. The app allows users to change temperatures, set schedules, adjust settings, and view usage. Using temperature, humidity, illumination, and occupancy sensors, the Smart AC Plug then keeps the room comfortable while cutting out energy waste.

Nature is also developing a smart learning algorithm that will learn your usage patterns and temperature preferences, and then control the AC unit automatically.

Nature is launching a Kickstarter campaign on May 9th. Until then, customers can sign up for communications on the company’s website: www.nature.global
