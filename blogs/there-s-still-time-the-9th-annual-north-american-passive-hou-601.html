There's Still Time: The 9th Annual North American Passive House Conference Kicks Off!

By Michael Knezovich · 10.09.2014

Passive building principles produce fantastically energy efficient and resilient buildings – and they provide one of the best paths to net zero. Passive building is quickly gaining adoption as a mainstream design method and energy standard.

The 9th Annual North American Passive House Conference, which will take place September 10-14 in the Bay Area, brings together a roster of experienced passive building practioners and leading policy makers. They will present case studies on a selection of cutting edge Bay Area passive projects, as well as projects from across the United States, Great Britain, Europe, and Japan.

See the full schedule here. 

Keynote speaker William Rose will provide a visionary keynote informed by a unique viewpoint on building science history. From there, the conferences will span two days, four tracks and 70+ unique presentations of the latest, greatest knowledge on passive building.

Sessions will range from very technical such as Dr. Hideki Shibaike's hygrothermal analysis of passive houses in hot humid environments in Japan, to the Builders' Hootenanny where folks who've built passive houses across North America talk about on-site challenges. Sessions will also include policy experts, including the U.S. Department of Energy's Sam Rashkin. And for raters and quality assurance professionals, RESNET Executive Director Steve Baden will be presenting.

The themes that run throughout the program are:

  • Passive building as the best route to net zero;
  • Large-scale multifamily and commercial passive buildings are growing strong; and
  • Climate-specific standards, including the release of the report from PHIUS/BSCs research project.

AIA continuing education units are approved for all sessions, and PHIUS Certified Passive House Consultants (CPHCs) will earn 14 CPHC CEUs for attending.

For more on the conference, visit http://naphc2014.phius.org.


About PHIUS

PHIUS is a 501(c)3 organization committed to making high-performance passive building the mainstream market standard. PHIUS is transforming the building sector by developing and promoting North American specific standards, practices and certifications for buildings, professionals and products to create structures that are durable, comfortable, healthy and super energy efficient.
