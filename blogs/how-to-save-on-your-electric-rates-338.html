How to Save On Your Electric Rates?

By Philip Hicks · 01.11.2012

If you live in a place where the state government has deregulated energy, then you have the opportunity to lower your electric rates simply by shopping for alternative electric suppliers.

Some people get confused about how switching the electric suppliers work for them on reducing the bills.  Here are some answers to the most frequently asked questions about lowering your electric rates by switching to alternative electric suppliers.

How do I lower my electric rates?

The easiest way to do this is by comparing the rates among different electric suppliers.  Keep in mind, that your electric rates will be affected by several factors, including:

  1. Whether you need energy for a home or business. Businesses tend to have more opportunity to save money on their electric rates, simply because there are more electric companies competing for their business.  Plus, big businesses tend to use more energy, which brings us to the next point.
  2. Your energy usage. Everyone has the opportunity to lower their electricity rates by switching to alternative electric suppliers. However, those who use more energy tend to see the biggest savings.
  3. The billing practices of the electric suppliers. You’ll need to check the contract to see whether the electric company offers fixed rates, variable rates or your choice.


Do I have to switch utility companies?

No. Even if you choose an alternative electric company to supply your energy, your existing utility company will continue to deliver your energy. Your existing utility company will be responsible for billing you, handling customer inquiries, responding to outages, building infrastructure and maintaining the infrastructure.


Will there be a disruption to the existing service?

No. The utility company and your new electric company will work together so that you can make the power switch appropriately.  You should see the new electric rates reflected on your utility bill within one or two billing cycles.


Are there other reasons for switching?

Absolutely there are. It is true, most people compare electric suppliers to lower their electricity bills. However, you can also compare electric suppliers to find one whom aligns with your values, such as a company which focuses on providing greener energy.


How do you compare the electric suppliers?

There are several websites available to compare the electric rates within your locality over the internet which provides the comprehensive list of supplier plans and rate information so that you can choose the best option for you. Therefore it is possible to compare the rates within few minutes through the web. All you have to do is pick one and make a comparison.
