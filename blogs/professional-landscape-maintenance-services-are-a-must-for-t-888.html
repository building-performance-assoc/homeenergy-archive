Professional landscape maintenance services are a must for the development of a society

By Richard Swanson · 13.10.2016

In the broadest sense landscape maintenance involves the art of keeping the surrounding landscape neat, clean, healthy and attractive. By using some modern tools, supplies, knowledge and physical labor the grounds keeper may plan or carry out the various plantings and harvesting,  garden care and path maintenance services, lightening, fencing, swimming pool care, runoff, drainage and irrigation services for protecting and improving the exterior landscape property in the most Eco friendly ways.

The various landscape designing companies aim at providing several landscape maintenance services. For this they hire a group of trained and licensed contractors who aim at providing creative designs and satisfy the customers to the utmost. The landscape maintenance services primarily include garden maintenance, horticulture maintenance and lawn maintenance.

Lawns are cut, edged and trimmed with the help of various lawn mowing machines. Gardens are also maintained by the continual process of weeding, edging, cultivation. The plants are also regularly trimmed and pruned up to 6 inches and the unwanted exotic seeds are removed to keep the garden property free of debris. These plants are regularly watered as well for their healthy growth. For those who prefer their own gardening, but still require garden pruning include grass cutting and blowing off the hard surfaces. The professional gardener charge some fee for this per cut or on a monthly basis.

Horticulture maintenance includes the use of various pesticides and insecticides for controlling weeds  while impacting the plants and the surrounding environment on the minimal level. By applying the fertilizers at the right time of the year they are able to get the most of the fertilizer application while using as little as possible to prevent environmental pollution. They also include a team of dedicated crew who regularly visit the site and look for any improvements that needs to be done on each property, both public and private throughout the year. The horticulturists also diagnose plant pests and diseases in the areas they serve. Once a plant disease is detected a plan is hatched to treat the identified plants, shrubs or trees to prevent the damage of these pollinators.

Horticulture services also place value in keeping the system of irrigation and drainage properly functioning for the customers. A well maintained and properly functioning irrigation system helps in keeping the lawns green and healthy for all seasons. They also provide irrigation maintenance services for homeowners as well as various commercially maintained properties like parks and roadsides as well. They also determine several inefficiencies like wasted water and provide measures accordingly. These horticulturists also offer other services like snow removal, path maintenance and run off by cutting down unwanted trees, and thus aim at landscape enhancement and not just landscape construction.

 Most people think that landscape maintenance services focus on only water distribution, drainage,  grading, plant selection and garden care, walls and fence construction and creating paths and walkways. It is a fact that several landscaping services offered by the companies ensure security for various residential houses as well as they examine the property and provides a list of measures to protect from the intruders like adding fences, trimming of large trees for preventing access to upper windows, removing unnecessary shrubs near the houses that are ideal spots of hiding. One disadvantage of these landscape services is that they are very costly.
