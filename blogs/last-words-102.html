Last Words

By Maggie Forti · 15.10.2010

With the Fall comes many changes; today I was reminded that within those changes some things remain the same.
One of my wonderful uncles, Joe, passed away this week at 90 years of age after a well-lived life! We celebrated him with many wonderful stories, one of which my co-workers felt I should share on the HE blog.

My uncle told his eldest, "Carol, I think I'm going to die tonight, I think this is it!"  She said, "Oh No, don't say that!"  Sometime later that evening he whispered, " Don't forget--the filter in the heater needs replacing! "  After which, Uncle Joe passed away peacefully in his sleep!
