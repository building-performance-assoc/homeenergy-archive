The Real Women of Home Performance: Amy Beley

By Macie Melendez · 13.01.2016

Amy Beley is an independent consultant in the home performance industry. She has a passion for connecting people and ideas. Most of her professional training comes from management consulting, which allows her to be adaptable to the needs of customers and clients. 

Macie Melendez: How did you get started in the home performance industry?

Amy Beley: I started working on a small DOE project called the Builders Challenge (Now called the Net Zero Energy Ready Challenge). It was a new construction program, and my first foray into the residential building world. I really enjoyed getting to interact with builders, raters, and contractors who are at the center of the industry. When the recession hit and the focus shifted to existing homes, I found my passion. What a complex industry the residential retrofit world is!

MM: How has your career evolved?

AB: I started out as more of a generalist—focused on federal programs that address energy needs at large. Over time, I’ve gravitated toward the more specific, really wanting to understand how businesses are affected by policy and program design. I wanted to give them more of a voice. I find myself craving the hands-on experience of a contractor. I’ve worked with many contractors in many parts of the country, and do my best to represent their interests in policy and program design. But at the end of the day, I can only imagine the day to day.

MM: When you started out, what was your biggest obstacle? How did you overcome it?

AB: Stepping into the spotlight, stepping into leadership. I thought it was much easier not to speak up or be a part of the conversation, because I didn’t think I had anything of value to contribute. It’s something I still battle with. Every day I remind myself that not only do I have something to say, I am working towards something that can have a real positive impact on the world. Sounds grandiose perhaps, but to change the world, all you have to do is change yourself.

MM: What is the most rewarding thing about your job?

AB: Working with contractors. Seeing them “get it”, and make more money doing it. Having the chance to help them be more profitable AND solve their customers problems.

MM: In your opinion, what are the biggest challenges for women in this industry?

AB: The biggest challenge is that there aren’t enough of us, frankly. I’ve seen a lot of young women come and go quickly. There’s no welcome committee for women, and there is still a persistence of gender bias in a lot of decisions made from the business level up to the national level. Who better to help you sell more to your target audience, which are women?

MM: What advice would you give to a woman starting out in the home performance industry?

AB: Find mentors. Connect with each other. Encourage one another. And find advocates. I’ve been so fortunate to have found many people (men and women) to support me in my career. I would not be where I am now without them.  We have a breakfast coming up at ACI New York in February, and we’ll make a bigger splash at the ACI National Conference in Austin.  I encourage your readers to participate in Women in Home Performance events!

 

Amy Beley can be contacted at amy@beley.org
