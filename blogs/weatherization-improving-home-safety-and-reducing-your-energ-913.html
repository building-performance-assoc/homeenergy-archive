Weatherization: Improving Home Safety and Reducing Your Energy Bill

By Mila Adamovica · 16.11.2016

Here, at PK Safety, we recently learned about a nationwide program instituted by the U.S. Department of Energy that promotes the use of clean and sustainable energy through weatherization funding. It’s the Weatherization Assistance Program. This came to our attention when a couple of new customers approached us from community action groups that specifically carry out weatherization services. These customers purchased gas detectors for their organizations, so we looked into it a bit to find out more about how they are helping their communities. We worked with the Illinois Home Weatherization Assistance Program and the B.C.M.W. Community Services. They found us after being advised to purchase RKI Instruments four-gas monitors that would bring them into OSHA compliance for their services and qualify them for their state’s funds. One of our Corporate Account Experts worked with them to help choose the right solution for gas detection in their projects, and recommended the GX-2009 4-Gas Monitor from RKI Instruments.

What Is Involved In a Weatherization Project?

In order for a building to be properly weatherized, it will need to undergo an audit and an assessment during which several elements in the home are being checked and assessed for energy use with the aim to pinpoint problem areas. These areas include home appliances, electronics, insulation, lighting, space heating and water heating/cooling, windows, doors, and roofs. Some upgrades that are most often needed in a weatherization retrofit include insulating walls, attics, water heaters and pipes, sealing doors, crawls spaces, basements, applying weather stripping and caulk, replacing doors, windows, walls and roofing, installing new HVAC and ducting, and even adding solar energy panels. 

Why Are Gas Monitors Needed for Weatherization Projects? 

Confined spaces are usually poorly ventilated and may contain insufficient oxygen or hazardous toxic gases. In 2015 the definition of a confined space was expanded to include attics and crawl spaces. Service providers encounter numerous safety risks when entering homes to accomplish weatherization projects. In order to qualify for federal and state funding through the Weatherization Assistance Program, the organizations that carry out these projects need to ensure they are OSHA-compliant. OSHA requires performing gas monitoring of confined spaces before entering them in order to prevent serious injury o

r death. Most commonly, there are cases where a worker could be exposed to hydrogen sulfide, carbon monoxide, or combustible gases that would poison them or leave them with a lack of oxygen. Since RKI Instruments company is a very dependable and respected brand of gas detection equipment, it makes perfect sense that the OSHA representative recommended these devices to the Illinois Home Weatherization Assistance Program for their gas detection needs. PK Safety carries different models of RKI gas detectors and accessories and is happy to help you find the right gas detection solution for your weatherization projects.

What Are The Benefits of Weatherization?

Weatherization improvements in buildings make a positive impact on environment through clean energy use and reduced energy consumption. Depending on the climate, you will need different levels of insulation, moisture control and ventilation. Proper insulation provides resistance to heat flow which will, in turn, lower the heating and the cooling costs, and make your residence more comfortable for you to live in. Not only is weatherization good for your energy bill, it helps solving safety issues caused by out-of-date appliances and HVAC systems.

Who is Eligible for Weatherization Assistance?

Weatherization Assistance resources are available in all states through the U.S. Department of Energy. According to DOE, more than 30 million U.S. families are eligible for weatherization services that are provided by the states’ local weatherization agencies. 

Families that receive Supplemental Security Income are automatically eligible for the weatherization. Not only house owners, but also renters who meet the corresponding criteria are eligible for these services if the landlord accepts the terms of the project. The Department of Energy issued important guidelines that emphasize that the priority must be given to the elderly, to the people with disabilities, and to the families with children. Income is one of the main factors affecting eligibility: those whose income falls below the "200% poverty level” are eligible.

Check your state and local government’s Weatherization Assistance Programs and your local power supply company’s website for more information. We are thankful to the organizations like B.C.M.W. Community Services or the Illinois Home Weatherization Assistance Program for promoting clean energy use.
