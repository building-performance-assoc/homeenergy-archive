10 Green Home Improvement Resolutions for 2013

By Rachel Kopp · 19.12.2012

It is more important now than ever to be conscious about our individual impact on the environment and there are lots of ways to become more energy-efficient without completely altering your lifestyle. Here are ten ways to get your 2013 New Year's Resolution list going.

1. Focus On Improving Your Heating System

The number-one source of energy consumption in the home is the cost of heating it in the winter. If your furnace is more than twenty years old you should seriously consider investing in a new one. If you're not due for a new piece of equipment, ensure that you always keep your filters clean and close off unused rooms. 

2. Seal Your Home

Outside climate conditions are the reason we control the climate inside our homes. Keep out the cold winter winds by weather stripping your doors and windows, and consider getting double or even triple-paned windows to allow less of the cold weather to seep into your home.

3. Use a Programmable Thermostat

Thermostats are no longer hard to read, and many different kinds of digital and easily programmable thermostats are currently dominating the market. Programmable thermostats allow you to decide how the heater or air conditioner will be used for different times of each day, allowing you to plan accordingly.

4. Evaluate Your Water Heater's Efficiency

Consider replacing a water heater that is more than fifteen or twenty years old, as newer models are designed to be more energy-efficient. Try putting an insulation jacket over the water heater and the pipes that connect it to discourage the loss of heat and make sure the temperature is set at 120 degrees Fahrenheit.

5. Insulate Your Entire Home

If your basement, attic, and/or garage is not yet insulated, consider an easy way to complete this task by using methods that won't require the remodel of the entire area, such as spray insulation.

6. Install Solar Panels

Though solar panels are an expensive solution to improve energy consumption, they are very effective in eating some of your monthly energy costs and reducing your carbon footprint. Begin with a converter and only a few solar panels to ease your energy usage. Once you have the converter, you can more easily install additional panels and gradually use more of that energy.

7. Wash Your Dishes By Hand

An incredible amount of energy is used each time the dishwasher is turned on to heat the water, splash it around and then heat the dishes to drying. Hand wash your dishes, let them air dry and ease up on the energy and water the dishwasher consumes.

8. Purchase and Use Energy Conscious Power Strips

There are several brands of power strips that allow you to stop the flow of energy to plugged-in equipment that is not being used. In addition to this, make sure your items are all unplugged from the wall when not in use.

9. Have Your Home Evaluated

Putting your house through and energy consumption evaluation will give you a harshly realistic picture of the energy your home uses, and will provide ways for you to improve how much you consume.

10. Resolve To Always Be Conscious of Your Energy Usage

The most powerful energy-saving tool is to be consistently conscious of the problem. If you're constantly aware of your own personal energy use, you will remember to turn off the lights, take shorter showers, and wear a sweater instead of turning on the heater.
