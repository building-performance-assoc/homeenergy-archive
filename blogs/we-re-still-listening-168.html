We're Still Listening

By Macie Schreibman · 30.08.2011

It’s officially been a month since our new website has launched, and we’re constantly improving its features.

Since the launch, we’ve added:

  • blog commenting functionality
  • more articles in each searchable category
  • 19 years worth of searchable article tags to help our readers find content easier
  • and many small, behind-the-scenes tweaks

Still to come is:

  • the ability for our readers to post their own blogs to our site
  • live Twitter feeds
  • discussion links from articles to Home Energy Pros
  • and much more

Is there something we are missing? Please let us know your thoughts on the new website – we’d love your feedback. Send comments, questions, and suggestions to publisher(at)homeenergy(dot)org.
