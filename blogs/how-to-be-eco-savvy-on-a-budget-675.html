How To Be Eco-savvy On A Budget

By Shawn Langston · 19.03.2015

How to Be Eco-Savvy On A Budget

Making simple changes in your day to day life can save you money, help simplify your life, and reduce the amount of waste produced. Green is definitely the new black. 

Here are some easy, beneficial ways to live an eco-friendly lifestyle without destructing your budget:

Turn the faucet off

Do you stare in the mirror as you brush your teeth in the morning with the water still running? If so, you’re part of the majority. Personally, I began to realize that I was leaving the water running for up to two minutes and started thinking about the amount of water going down the drain. It seemed like a small amount to me, but not letting your water run continuously is said to save up to 10 gallons of water per day and it’s estimated that 684 gallons of water go down the drain a year just from a person brushing their teeth. Shutting off your water is one of the easiest ways to save water. Do yourself a favor and turn the faucet off when brushing your teeth! 

Tankless Water Heater

Want to have more space, a $1,500 tax credit, and save 40% on energy costs? Get a tankless water heater. They are more energy-efficient and your electric bills diminish greatly. The technology produces less CO2 than a traditional gas or electric water heater. Companies such as Olshan Plumbing install tankless water heaters at an affordable price.  The tankless units can be installed on essentially any wall inside or outside your home. Going tankless is a WIN.    

Unplug

I'm guessing you have about 10 or more appliances/electronics plugged in right now that aren't being used. It's estimated that these phantom power users can cost up to $100 each year, just to be plugged in! There are two simple ways to help you save money and energy: simply unplug whatever you aren't using or buy a power strip for all cords and turn the switch off when not in use. This is an easy way to save 5-10% on your electric bill.  The small things add up to make the big!

Plastic isn't always fantastic

It's said that the average family accumulates 60 plastic bags after four trips to the store. Bring your own bags to the store! Many bigger chain businesses as well as local grocery stores sell reusable bags at an affordable cost. California is the first and only state thus far to ban single-use plastic bags in grocery stores, pharmacies, and liquor stores. Plastic bags are already being phased out in some places. It's common sense to invest in reusable bags and cheap (usually costing $.99 to 3.00). Getting into the habit of bringing your own bag can be enough to solve the problem of overuse of our resources.  

Get a Glass Water Bottle

Companies such as Life Factory have come up with some pretty awesome glass water bottles. There are many benefits of using glass instead of plastic bottles such as being safe and non-toxic, easy to clean, and the water tastes better, no joke.  Here are some glass bottles with bright and personalized colors. Bring your own coffee cup to work

Americans throw away an average of 25 million paper cups a year. The average American working in an office uses about 500 disposable cups a year. Find yourself a mug that suites you and become the leader of the office. If you drink coffee or tea every day, you have a lot of options for an awesome mug.
