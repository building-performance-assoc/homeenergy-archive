Roofing tips from a roofing contractor

By Zylen Colt · 22.04.2015

When the recession hit, Kevin Kuchel was in the construction business building homes and condos. It didn’t take long for the economic downturn to run him out of business. He went two years without a project and without a paycheck. Then after a particularly nasty storm, he found himself filling in his downtime repairing roof damage. He decided then to switch to the roofing business and hasn’t looked back. Here is some advice Kuchel has for homeowners when it comes to taking care of their roofs.

Be vigilant in looking for damage

Ideally, you should be having your roof inspected by a professional regularly so that any problems that develop don’t go unnoticed. Because most roofers offer a free inspection, there’s no reason why homeowners can’t have their roofs professionally inspected from time to time. Kuchel reminds homeowners that they don’t have to wait for a specific time of year to have a professional inspect their roof. In fact, homeowners should be keeping an eye out for signs of damage year round. Some signs of roof damage are pretty obvious like when water is pouring into your home through your ceiling. But typically, roof damage can be spotted before it’s catastrophic if homeowners know what to look for.

Homeowners should keep an eye out for damaged or missing shingles. Any area where shingles have come loose or are broken is an area where water can potentially get into the home. Homeowners should also watch for any discoloration on their roofs, especially on the north side were algae is prone to grow. Though algae doesn’t really pose much of a threat to the integrity of the roof, it is unsightly. If algae growth is a big problem, homeowners can have zinc strips installed that stop algae growth.

Finally, homeowners can take care of their roof by taking care of their rain gutters. Gutters that become clogged with debris pose a number of problems for roofs. Homeowners can go a long way in preventing roof damage by simply making sure that the gutters remain clear of debris and that they are securely attached to the home. Homeowners should also watch for granule loss which typically manifests itself in the rain gutters. When shingles began to lose their granules, it’s going to be time for a new roof soon.
