Tips on Buying an Air Conditioner for Your Home

By Katherine Ogilvie · 20.02.2015

Air Conditioning experts EOC Services who manufacture, install and service in Peterborough and the surrounding area have issued their guide on what to consider when thinking about investing in air conditioning for your home.

Firstly, locating a reputable supplier who will be able to answer any specific queries you have is always a good start. If you are looking for something in particular then you may wish to speak with a specialist to ensure the system will meet certain specifications you are after. 

When speaking with an expert, have in your mind a good idea about what kind of size unit you are after, what kind of budget you are working with and what benefits you’re after. Also, using the internet to check the reviews of certain units can be helpful as long as you take them with a pinch of salt.

Any home use air conditioning system should be properly sized for the amount of square footage that your home has. Efficiency is often stated in BTU output, meaning that the higher the BTU rating the unit carries, the more efficient you can expect it to be. BTU often ranges between 5,000 up to 8,000 for a home use unit.

One of the key features of a home use air conditioner that you may wish to take into account during the selection process could be having a night mode. Night mode slows the compressor down at night so that the noise level is drastically reduced. You may even want to consider a unit with a complex timer system that would allow you to control the temperature during hours of residency and for when you are not there so that you can maximise saving on your household bills. Another key feature may perhaps be a remote control, most home users are happy without this function and often find a timer useful enough, however for some that have specific uses for rooms in their home; a remote can come in handy. For example, for those who operate as a business for home and find themselves in a home office for long periods of time or for those who have a home gym.

If you are certain of your investment in a home air conditioning unit then you will also need to take stock of how often the unit will need to be maintained. This is often overlooked buy buyers. More importantly, maintenance is crucial if you wish to keep up the validity of your warrant. Most owner manuals will indicate when this is due but it is worth thinking this through during the buying process as well because some units need more maintenance than others. If you are unsure, this is where communicating with a specialist can help. Generally speaking, units should be inspected once a year so that belts, coils and other working parts can be inspected. As a point of note, if or when you have had the unit installed, make sure not to place it near plants or foliage that could potentially grow into or near the unit and that filters are changed on a regular basis.
