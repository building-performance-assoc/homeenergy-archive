The Energy Conservatory Unveils the Future of Digital Pressure Gauges

By Michelle Peterson · 02.07.2015

The Energy Conservatory (TEC) recently announced its new digital pressure gauge and displayed a prototype at the ACI National Home Performance Conference in New Orleans. TEC’s gauge helps users diagnose pressure problems in buildings to improve energy efficiency and minimize utility costs.

The new gauge is one year in the making, with a strong foundation in research and insights from TEC’s customers. Features of the gauge include:

  • Capacitive touch screen, not resistive: The gauge responds to touch, without the need to apply pressure, making it easy to use on the job.
  • Compatible with all TEC products: All TEC products will work with the new gauge, including all new products TEC releases in the future. Users have the option to install updates from TEC, equipping their gauge with new products.
  • Future-proof technology: Users will be able to update the software, adding more features and functionality as needed.

Additional features include a full-color screen, built-in Wi-Fi, built-in USB, mini USB and Ethernet port, field replaceable, rechargeable batteries and a custom-built operating system. 

TEC’s new gauge will be available for purchase in late 2015. Visit TEC’s blog to learn more or contact Kim Tanner, marketing communications specialist, at KTanner@energyconservatory.com or (612) 254-2161.
