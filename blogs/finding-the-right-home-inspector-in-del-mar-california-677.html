Finding the Right Home Inspector in Del-Mar California

By Sam Cohen · 25.03.2015

When you think you have found the perfect house for yourself in Del-Mar, it might be true, but your untrained eyes may be missing a few underlying factors which only a home inspector may be able to find out for you. You can find the help of a professional inspector with a top  Del-Mar real estate agent. Under that perfectly decorated house may be  poor insulation or below standard electrical installations. Do not put a home inspection in your DIY list. Leave it to the pros and spend a few more hundred bucks on a professional home inspector. Based upon your inspection you may

  1. Opt out of purchasing the house
  2. Seek repair options
  3. Ask for credit towards the repairs
  4. Negotiate reduction in purchase price of the house

Find a home inspector

Always look for a qualified professional. You may be tempted to hire a person who charges far less money but at the end you may also be getting as poor a service as his charges. So, consider the following:

  • License: The basic requirement for the person you hire is to have a license or registration to work as an inspector.
  • Insurance: Check if the inspector has any insurance to cover errors and omissions and liability and see the proof of insurance for yourself.
  • Training: Find out what training school the inspector got his training from. Although formal training is optional but if he has got any training then find out if it is from a recognized school.
  • Experience: This is a very important factor in selecting a home inspector. However, years of experience may not be important but the total number of home inspections that have been completed by the professional. Ask about the number of inspections he has completed in a year. 200 sounds like a good number.
  • Pursuing further education: Skillsets get updated every year and if your professional inspector is continuing his education it is a good sign that he is serious about his profession. Your top Del-Mar realtors may be able to help you find such an inspector if you ask for their assistance.
  • Memberships: Seek an inspector who is well–trained and experienced and associated with an organization that takes care of such matters and has been in business for long. Such inspectors are likely to do a good job than others who lack experience or charge quite a low sum of money. Do your research properly before finalizing one.
  • Inspection hours: A thorough inspection of a fairly average sized home should take the inspector approximately 2-4 hours to complete. Also, you should be present at the time of inspection and if your presence is refused then maybe you would like to find another inspector.
  • Miscellaneous: Once you almost finalize a deal with your real estate agent in Del-Mar and have zeroed down on an inspector, ask if the inspector has any additional qualifications. You may ask if he is qualified to do radon testing. If you already have any service in mind make sure you ask the inspector ahead of time if he or his company will be offering that service.
  • Pricing: A good inspector will naturally demand more money owing to experience and training while a not-so-good inspector will charge lesser amount for obvious reasons. It is totally up to you how much money you are willing to spend or can afford. If you can, then it is advisable that you choose an organization that is experienced and has well-trained inspectors. In short, you get your money’s worth, so find out how much you can afford or are willing to spend on an inspector.
