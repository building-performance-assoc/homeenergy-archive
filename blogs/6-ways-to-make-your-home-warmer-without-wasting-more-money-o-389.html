6 Ways To Make Your Home Warmer Without Wasting More Money On Electricity

By Bill Walter · 07.03.2013

Would you prefer to live somewhere hot rather than somewhere freezing cold? I think most of us would and it's why we don't go on holiday during winter. There's something magical about being in a warm climate and it makes us feel great. It's especially good when you're walking around your holiday apartment and you don't need to worry about feeling like your toes are going to fall off.

It would be great if you could replicate this at home, but unfortunately not all of us can afford to have our heating on full-blast all the time. How would you like to make some changes to your home that would make you feel as warm as you could ever possibly want? Here's some great ways to get started that don't have anything to do with paying more on your energy bills.

Paint your exterior walls

You want your walls to absorb as much heat as possible because it means your home will be warmer without you doing anything, but first you need to give it a good paint. Dark colors absorb the heat much easier, so if your walls are white at the moment you might want to go a few shades closer to black. It won't turn your home into an inferno, but combined with everything else will produce great results.

Add wall insulation

Your walls aren't the greatest things in the world for keeping the heat inside. You can warm your home all you like, but the heat will eventually creep out. The only thing you can do to stop it is by putting something in its path so it can't escape easily. That's where wall insulation comes into play. It's not too expensive to have put into your walls, but you'll definitely see a difference in your power bills once you do.

Install a log fire

How would you like to go outside and chop your own wood? It means you are getting huge amounts of heat for free. Even if you bought the wood instead you would still save money compared to what you're paying for electricity at the moment. If you've never watched a movie at night beside a log fire you don't know what you're missing. It's definitely going to be a highlight of your week.

Use thick blankets

What do you have on your bed at the moment? Sometimes it's very cold at night and when we get into bed it's always a bit chilly. It's definitely not a nice feeling and you could even wake up in the middle of the night because you can't feel your toes. What you need is a big thick blanket for on your bed and you'll never be cold again. You can also keep one in the living room because you might want to lie on the couch while watching television.

Lay fluffy rugs

Walking on cold carpets is not nice and it means you're always having to put on your slippers. You shouldn't have to wear footwear in your own home when you want to relax. If you lay some fluffy rugs down on all your carpets it will feel great when you walk over them and it will also keep your feet nice and warm. As an added bonus it can also change the look of a room which is always a nice thing.

A high pressure shower

One of the warmest places in your home is definitely the shower, when you're standing under there with the hot water running there's nothing quite like it. You probably don't get to spend as much time in there as you'd like to when the water only dribbles out at a snail's pace. If you install a high pressure shower it's a lot different and you actually look forward to washing more than you probably should.
