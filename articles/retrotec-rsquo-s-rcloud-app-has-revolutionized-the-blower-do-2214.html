Retrotec&rsquo;s rCloud App has Revolutionized the Blower Door and Duct Testing Industries

Published 01.11.2017

[SPONSORED CONTENT] The method used to run tests and record results has remained unchanged since the beginning of the blower door and duct testing industries. This process involves setting up and manually running tests and recording results with pencil and paper or manually entering results into an electronic device. As the industry has grown, professionals have been seeking new ways to improve their testing and reporting technique. Retrotec recognized this need which lead to the creation of rCloud: an app that geolocates, automatically runs tests to various standards, records and securely stores data and generates reports.

This new testing platform is the first of its kind. The user-friendly interface and time-saving attributes suggest that this technology will change the way we test buildings, ducts, ventilation systems and more.

rCloud allows anyone with a mobile device or tablet to perform a fully automated blower door or duct test. The rCloud app provides secure and accurate data that includes the location where the test was performed, local weather, set-up conditions, photos, and the calibration date of the gauge. In some markets, rCloud will provide existing housing data. rCloud then performs blower door or duct tests to common standards, and creates secure compliance reports that can be shared immediately and viewed from anywhere.

Functionality, Integration & Versatility

The release of rCloud concludes eight years of product development that allows Retrotec’s DM32 manometer to be used to its highest potential. The DM32 is the world’s first WiFi touchscreen smart gauge which is compatible with more than just Retrotec’s line of blower doors and duct testers. Testers using TEC fans can still run equipment with the DM32 WiFi smart gauge and a Speed Control Adapter. The rCloud app connects directly to Retrotec’s DM32 WiFi smart gauge. Once connected, the blower door or duct testing equipment is set up as normal and the rCloud app is opened on a smartphone or tablet. Select the type of test and rCloud does the rest.

Upgrading Gauges for Compatibility

To make the rCloud platform more accessible to everyone in the industry, Retrotec has introduced its trade-in program so that testers using a TEC DG-700 Pressure & Flow Gauge can upgrade to the Retrotec DM32 smart gauge for an introductory price.

Accessibility and Shared Usage

The rCloud app is already available for iOS, Android and Windows 8.1 & 10 and is free to download. Users can create an account and begin using the software immediately.

Companies can create a business account and add in multiple users, allowing for administrators to oversee results of all projects performed. Company accounts can have multiple users and the reports can be viewed in the app or online.

One of the most powerful benefits of rCloud is the ability to see all the test locations on a map. Select any location and see all the test performed at that location then select a test to see and share the compliance report.

Gaining the Benefits of Security, Privacy, and Testing Assurance

The rCloud app provides quality assurance for the user and entire team. Data is collected securely, time & date-stamped, geo-tagged, and uploaded to cloud storage. Data storage is secured through Microsoft Azure, and the data is secure the moment the test is complete. Even while the data is stored in the device it is encrypted and cannot be altered.

No more pencil and paper or lost test results. Access compliance reports via the rCloud web portal, find your report, save and share as a PDF.

Meeting Testing Standards

The rCloud app currently includes testing standards for International Energy Conservation Code 2009, 2012 and 2015, RESNET and California Title 24 standards. This includes duct testing and single and multipoint blower door tests. Additional standards, such as RESNET/ANSI 380 and European test standards, are also underway.
