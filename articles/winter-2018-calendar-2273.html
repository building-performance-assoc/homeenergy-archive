Home Energy Media Partnerships Launched in 2016

In 2016, Home Energy launched exciting media partnerships with the foremost trade organizations in the home performance and green building industry. These include the Home Performance Coalition (formerly ACI), Building Performance Institute (BPI), and Efficiency First. These partnerships mean increased subscription discounts for HPC and EEBA attendees, BPI certified pros, and Efficiency First members, and increased distribution for advertisers. Home Energy is also a media sponsor for the following events: Better Buildings: Better Business; Energy Design; Insulation Contractors Assoc. of America; North American Passive House Conference; North American Passive House Network.
