Home Performance Coalition Acquires <em>Home Energy</em>

Published 09.12.2018

Dear Subscribers,

We have exciting news! The Home Performance Coalition (HPC) is acquiring Home Energy magazine. As longtime collaborators, we’ve been working closely together for over a year in anticipation of this new growth opportunity. Now, we will be part of the same team with Home Energy magazine continuing as the publishing arm of the Home Performance Coalition.

The magazine will continue to provide the same high-quality building science-based journalism you’ve come to expect, but it will also offer more educational materials, training guides, and conference discounts, as well as business development and networking opportunities. As we transition to a united association, we welcome your input and look forward to providing you with these additional benefits.

Your subscription will continue as usual, but if you normally pay by credit card, your bank statement will now show Home Performance Coalition. If you renew by check, please send your checks made payable to:
