Letters

Published 26.10.2014

Last month’s cover story featured 30 home performance professionals 30 years of age or younger to celebrate our 30th year in publishing. We are very grateful for our readers who have been with us for the past three decades, as well as for all of our new ones, and we sincerely hope that our recent cover story helped to show others that our industry has a bright future.

We had fun getting to know the next generation of home performance professionals, and we’ve received a lot of feedback on the article. Following are some of our favorite responses.
